  Full mana: 2892 in 7 shells. 40s siege from full mana: 6347.
  Pool is 1661 mana, R cooldown 0.69s. Scored caps: 200 on half mana, 250 on a full bar. Fastest tank kill in the table: cap 150.
  On a half bar, a 250-mana shell refreshes the stack timer and the tank lives. Let the 8s timer fall off. The second volley is the kill.

  MANA LADDER (no refund):
  Shells 1–4 cost 50+100+150+200 = 500. Shells 5–7 add 250+300+350 = 900.
  Shells 8–10 add 400+450+500 = 1350. Damage per mana falls off immediately.
  Archangel and Seraph refund 25% of that. Tear refunds 10%. Nothing else does.
  Rank 2 R (level 9, ~8:00) is a 1.0s base cooldown. Malignance's 20
  ultimate haste already puts that under a second. Further haste boots
  add almost no shells. Mana is what adds shells.

  WHY THESE ITEMS:
  • Malignance is the artillery item: 500 mana, 20 ultimate haste, Hatefog
    (60+5% AP per second and 10 MR shred while you keep casting).
  • Horizon Focus is 10% damage on a hit from 600+ range. R is 1100/1300/1500.
    The amp is on every shell and every burn.
  • Liandry is 2% max HP per second, refreshed by R. It is the long tank fight.
    It gives no mana and no haste.
  • Void Staff is 40% magic pen. Spellslinger adds 18 flat and 8% more,
    and it cannot be bought before 10:00.
  • Infinity Orb's 20% bonus starts below 40% HP, the same line where R doubles.
    A healthy tank never sees it.
  • Luden's isolated echo (7.3) is 175+14% AP every 9 seconds. One or two procs.
    It does not refund mana. Tier-3 mage items no longer grant 7% pen.
  • Seraph refunds a quarter of every shell and holds 1200 mana, but the
    charge takes minutes and the shells are still weak without pen and Horizon.
  • Max Q. Caustic Spittle is 20–32% MR shred for 4 seconds and a 65% AP hit.
    W is the on-hit build, which is a different sim.
  • E stays uncast in this pattern. Its mana is another shell, and the
    target in this sim already stands in the artillery.

  Seraph on this minute: half 2310 (7R), full 2802 on the bar (10R) from 2361 mana. The refund buys shells. Hatefog plus the burn still clears more of a half bar.

  At 20:00 the highest single minute is Malig → Spellslinger → Void (237.7 vs 228.7). From 6:00 to 20:00 this path still leads (173.3 vs 171.9 for Malig → Spellslinger → Void).
