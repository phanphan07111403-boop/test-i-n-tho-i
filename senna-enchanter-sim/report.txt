ITEM ROLES (why order matters)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Ardent  2400  50 AP, 8% HSP, NO haste. Buff: 30% AS + 25 on-hit for 6s
                 on you AND the ally you Q. Senna autos, so she uses it too.
                 AS also refunds Q CD (1s per auto) despite her 0.4 AS ratio.
  Helia   2400  40 AP, 20 AH, 200 HP. 30% of champ damage stored
                 (cap 80–250 by level). Senna Q damages THEN heals —
                 store and dump in one cast. Autos between Qs fill shards.
                 20 AH + auto refund = much shorter Q interval.
                 Shares Bandleglass + Kindlegem with Harmonic.
  Whisper 2400  8% HSP + Harmony (0.5% of max mana as HSP, 25% mana refund).
                 Tear stacks transfer; 700 bonus mana → Diadem of Songs.
                 Diadem: 0.8% max mana HPS in combat to lowest-HP ally.
                 Senna is less mana-hungry than Sona (15s Q, auto refunds).
  Harmonic 2500 40 AP, 20 AH, 200 HP, NO HSP. Chain 30% heal / 35% shield
                 to a second ally (or extra on the ADC in 2v2).
                 Same components as Helia — buying one delays the other
                 by a full item.

  20-min support gold finishes ~3 legendaries. The 4th is often leftover.
  First two slots decide the game. Do not spend them on the two Kindlegem
  items back-to-back.

VERDICT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Best order: Ardent → Helia → Harmonic → Whisper
  Time-weighted buff/impact: 10393  |  gold-eff: 804.4 impact / 1k gold
  Ardent online:   ~8:00   (ADC + Senna 30% AS / 25 on-hit)
  Circlet/Diadem: NOT finished by 20:00 — 4th item on a 20-min clock
  Helia online:    ~14:00   (Q store+dump + 20 AH for Q interval)
  Harmonic online: ~18:00   (chain to the second ally in 5v5)

  Vs double-Kindlegem first (Helia → Harmonic → Ardent → Whisper): +52% impact at 12:00 (4997 vs 3278), +60% at 16:00.
  Ardent-first vs Whisper-first: 8:00 3688 vs 2091  |  16:00 7124 vs 7216.
  Ardent-first vs Helia-first:   8:00 3688 vs 2386 (+55%). Helia is closer on Senna than on Sona (same-cast Q dump) but still loses the 8:00 spike.

  WHY THIS ORDER:
  • Patch 7.3 buffed marksmen (200% crit). Ardent is a flat 30% AS /
    25 on-hit at 2400g. It buffs the ADC AND Senna's own autos, and
    the extra AS refunds Q CD (1s per auto). First-item Ardent is the
    8–14 dragon-window fight buff.
  • Helia second adds 20 AH so Q interval drops (haste + auto refund)
    and every Q becomes a Helia dump. 0 HSP; Ardent already brought 8%.
  • Third (Harmonic) is the long-game slot — only live if the match
    lasts. 20-min support gold usually finishes exactly three legendaries.
  • Harmonic shares Bandleglass + Kindlegem with Helia. Buying both
    before Ardent/Circlet means the ADC never gets the 7.3 fight buff.
  • Tear rush before Ardent delays the 2400g spike by one component.
    Skip Tear until Circlet is next if you are rushing Ardent.

  RECOMMENDED PURCHASE ORDER (20-min enchanter Senna, Wild Rift 7.3):
  1) Spectral Sickle → Black Mist Scythe  — quest gold / 10 AH
  2) Boots of Speed                       — don't delay the first 2400g
  3) Ardent Censer                        — ADC + Senna 30% AS / 25 on-hit
  4) Ionian Boots of Lucidity             — 15 AH, shorter Q interval
  5) Echoes of Helia                      — 20 AH + Q store-and-dump
  6) Harmonic Echo                        — chain heal/shield in 5v5s
  7) Whispering Circlet → Diadem          — HSP + mana refund + combat HPS

  Swap Ardent → Staff of Flowing Waters (40 AP / 15 AH for 6s) if your
  carry is AP (Kai'Sa hybrid, Corki, AP Ezreal). Same slot, same timing.

  Trap: Helia → Harmonic. Same recipe, no ADC buff, Ardent never lands
  for the 12:00 dragon. Shared Kindlegem is the least gold-efficient pair.
  Trap: Harmonic first in lane. Chain has no second target; 2500g for
  AH that Helia also buys — and Helia actually converts Senna's autos.
  Trap: Tear rush into item 1. Senna is not as mana-hungry as Sona;
  400g delays the first legendary. Only rush Tear if Circlet is 1st/2nd.
