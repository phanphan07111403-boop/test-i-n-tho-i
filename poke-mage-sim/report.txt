VERDICT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Best poke mage for HIGHEST RANGE + SHORT COOLDOWN:  Kog'Maw (AP) R
  • Living Artillery: 1800 range, 0.86s cycle @22:00, 9 shots / 12s.
  • Range-frequency 2103 vs Ziggs 524, Hwei QW 459, Xerath Q 303, Nidalee 386.
  • Expected squish damage / 12s: Kog 1351, Ziggs 1171 (-13%), Xerath 579.

  IF YOU WANTED SPEAR-ONLY NIDALEE, LOCK ONE OF THESE INSTEAD:
  1) AP Kog'Maw  — the actual 'spam long-range skillshot' champion.
     R is 1800 with a 1s CD (mana is the real limiter, not cooldown).
     Hide in fog, drop R on feet. You already have a Malignance sim.
  2) Ziggs       — best BASIC-ability Q spam. 1400 bounce, 4s CD
     (5 bombs / 12s). Siege, waveclear, turret. Easier than Xerath.
  3) Xerath      — longest *identity* as an artillery mage (1450 Q +
     5000 R). Range is real; 'short cooldown' is not: max Q still
     costs 1.75s charge, cycle 4.78s, only 3 beams / 12s.
  4) Hwei QW     — longest basic spell (1900) but 1s delay + 6s CD.
     Great poke, not a spam button.

  NOT THE ANSWER:
  • Nidalee Q: 1500 range looks close to Xerath, but 6s CD, ~32% hit, and Hunt wants cougar. RF 386 vs Kog 2103.
  • Karthus Q: shortest CD in the table (0.86s, 14 ticks) but 875 range — that's a melee-adjacent circle, not artillery.
  • Ezreal Q: spam (6 shots, RF 539) at 1200 — ADC/hybrid, not a mage, and not highest range.
  • Jayce Q+E: 1600 gated, 8s CD, physical. Cannon poke, not a mage.
  • Lux / Mel / Vel'Koz: fine poke mages, shorter range or longer CD.

  HOW TO READ THE TWO WINNERS:
  • Want the NUMBER (range × 1/CD):     AP Kog'Maw R.
  • Want a mage whose Q is the spam:    Ziggs.
  • Want to never enter vision:         Xerath (Q from fog + R).
  Do not lock Nidalee for this job.
