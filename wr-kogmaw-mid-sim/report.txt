PRESS RULE
  Spam is allowed only inside the kill line. Above the line the glob is chip,
  and if they are under 50% HP that chip also puts Dark Harvest on a 20s CD.
  Chain casts (Echo on cooldown) are the number that has to hold for EVERY R.
  Wait 1.0s between takedowns so the Harvest reset is up. Do not queue early.

RUNE PAGE
  Keystone: Dark Harvest
  Domination: Eyeball Collection (3 AP × takedowns, cap 8). Other dom slots
  do not add artillery damage (no dash for Sudden Impact, R does not impair).
  Off-tree: Coup  — best of Axiom (+8% ult, R is AoE so not 12%),
  Coup de Grace (+8% damage below 40% HP), Scorch (21–49 magic, 1s later).

  WHY THIS PATH
  • Full-HP Living Artillery is 25% AP. One glob does not remove a healthy
    bar. The kill is the under-40% formula (200/280/360 + 50% AP), Infinity
    Orb's 20% on that same bracket, Coup de Grace, and Dark Harvest.
  • Flat pen and Void's 40% pen are what make that execute clear the
    remaining HP. Horizon Focus is +10% only after you have already hit
    the target, so it misses a one-R kill. At 18:00 the Horizon path's ADC
    line is 22.9% against 33.9% here.
  • Liandry does not move a one-cast kill. At 18:00 that foil sits at
    21.9% ADC. Hatefog's 10 MR shred hits the next glob, not this one.
  • Luden's Echo raises the first cast and is down for 9s. The score
    uses the naked follow-up, because spam means R2 and R3 still have to kill.
  • Deathcap is not finished on even-mid gold by 18:00. The rod in the
    inventory is the start of it, not the 30% multiplier.
  • 11:00 the ADC picks up an MR component and the line drops 6.1 points until the next pen item.
  • Q shred adds 1.1 points at 18:00. Pen has already eaten most of
    the ADC's MR, so the bush spit is a small bump, not the build.
  • Next path (Storm → IO → Slinger → Void) is ADC 18.6% at 8:00, 22.8% at 12:00, 32.8% at 16:00. This path is 23.7% / 22.7% / 32.8%.
    Those paths finish the same pen stack. The gap is earlier: Infinity Orb is the execute amp, so it is the first legendary.

  HOW TO SPAM
  1. Sit in fog. Do not open on a full HP bar. That glob is chip, and under
     50% HP it also puts Dark Harvest on a 20s cooldown.
  2. Press R only when their current HP% is at or under the chain line.
  3. That cast kills. Harvest resets to 1s. Wait that second, then the
     next champion under their own line dies to the next R.
  4. If Harvest is down, use the dry line or do not cast.
  5. Skip bruisers until their printed percent. One R is not a tank button.
  6. Mana ramps 50 → 500 if you chain inside 8s. Three kill shots cost
     50+100+150. Base mana pays for that. It does not pay for chipping
     one full bar down to the line by yourself.

  Skill order: R at 5/9/13, then Q, then E, W last.
  W max is the on-hit build. This one does not auto from fog.
