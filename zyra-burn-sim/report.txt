VERDICT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Best path (peak burn WITH duration lock): Liandry → Rylai → BF (Best 20m)
  Duration-weighted avg: 13.0 | Peak raw burn DPS: 23.2
  Rylai uptime lock online: ~16:00
  Note: Liandry + Rylai + Blackfire rarely all finish in a 20-min support game.

  WHY THIS PEAKS AND STILL HAS DURATION:
  • Liandry = 2% max HP / sec for 3s — THE harass burn (plants refresh it)
  • Blackfire = (20 + 2% AP)/s + 20 AH — second burn + more plant spam
  • Plants auto-hit 6s (W resets) — spam trees, accuracy does not matter
  • Rylai 30% slow = enemies cannot walk out before 3s burn finishes
  • 20-min support gold forces a choice: double-burn OR Rylai lock — not both early
  • Winner prioritizes Liandry spike (~9:00) then Rylai (~16:00) so burn works.

  RECOMMENDED PURCHASE ORDER (20-min plant-spam Zyra support):
  1) Spectral Sickle → Black Mist Scythe
  2) Boots of Speed + Fated Ashes (~5:00 first burn)
  3) Haunting Guise → Liandry's Torment (~9:00 PEAK harass spike)
  4) Boots of Mana
  5) Rylai's Crystal Scepter (LOCK duration — mandatory for burn to work)
  6) Blackfire Torch only if game goes past ~18–20
  7) Long game: Deathcap / Void / Morello

  Trap: Liandry → Blackfire skips Rylai → higher paper burn, enemies walk out.
  Trap: Rylai first delays Liandry → you lose the mid-game harass peak.
