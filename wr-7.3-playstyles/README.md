# Wild Rift 7.3 — Top 10 playstyles that benefit most, by role

Patch **7.3 (Center Stage, 2026-09-22)** is not a small balance pass. Three systems move at once:

1. **Marksman identity split** — crit damage **175% → 200%**, Infinity Edge **230%**, abilities that now scale with crit chance *and* extra crit damage, new first items (Yun Tal, Stormrazor, Hexoptics C44, Fiendhunter Bolts), Magnetic Blaster deleted.
2. **Jungle diversity** — Smite is a **stat-scaling true-damage burn** (AD / AP / armor / MR / HP). Tank and mage junglers clear without being forced into Sunfire. Objectives are tankier; Smite upgrades to **1400**.
3. **Siege + support specialization** — permanent turret plating, Crystalline Overgrowth, melee **+20% turret damage**, enchanter items stripped down to heal/shield power, Mandate/Helia rebuilt for CC mages and poke-heal.

This list ranks **playstyles that 7.3 itself lifts**, not “who was already S+ on 7.2c.” Ezreal, Yone, Smolder, and Senna were strong last patch and mostly **did not** get this patch’s toys. Rankings are patch-note inference on day 1 — no 7.3 ranked sample yet.

Build orders are the **identity spike**, not a full situational tree. Swap LDR / Mortal / Wit’s End / Shieldbow when the lobby demands it.

Source: [official 7.3 patch notes](https://wildrift.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/wild-rift-patch-notes-7-3/).

## ADC

Crit ADCs are the biggest winners in the game. On-hit is the second winner (Statikk Shiv bounces apply on-hit; Rageblade no longer bans crit). Jhin / Senna / Yasuo / Yone had their crit modifiers **cut** so they do not fully eat the 200% crit buff.

| # | Playstyle | Best champs | Core spike | Why 7.3 |
|---|-----------|-------------|------------|---------|
| 1 | Trap-headshot sniper | Caitlyn | Yun Tal or Stormrazor → IE → Hexoptics C44 | Base AD 54→60. Headshot, traps, and Ace in the Hole all scale with crit chance + extra crit damage. High natural AS so she buys AD/crit, not more AS. Hexoptics is her new Mag Blaster: range on takedown + far-target amp. |
| 2 | Shove-and-siege ricochet | Sivir | Yun Tal → IE → RFC / PD | Fleet of Foot **31–45 → 55–70 MS**. Boomerang Blade and Ricochet rewritten to scale with crit. Permanent plates + Crystalline Overgrowth + stronger cannon/super minions are *her* map. |
| 3 | Bomb-jump all-in | Tristana | Stormrazor / Yun Tal → IE → Fiendhunter or Collector | Explosive Charge active now multiplies with crit chance + crit damage. Rocket Jump and Buster Shot pick up **bonus AD**. Base AD 54→60. |
| 4 | Feather-recall crit | Xayah | Yun Tal → IE → Fiendhunter Bolts | Bladecaller is now `(base + 50% bAD) × (1 + crit terms)`. Feather extra damage 20%→25%. Fiendhunter is built for ult windows (Featherstorm). |
| 5 | Stealth-ambush burst | Twitch | Yun Tal / Stormrazor → Fiendhunter → IE | Full kit rework: **AS after leaving camouflage**, Contaminate split AD/AP, no more max-stack AS steroid. Fiendhunter is named for Twitch/Zeri/Yunara ults (3 guaranteed crits / true damage if already critting). |
| 6 | Lightslinger tempo crit | Lucian | Essence Reaver or Stormrazor → IE → Collector / Shieldbow | Culling bullet count = `20 + 20×crit + extra-crit term`. Piercing Light **100% bonus AD**. Base AD 60. He is a crit champion now, not “always ER into Mag Blaster.” |
| 7 | On-hit true-damage duelist | Vayne | Kraken / BotRK → Rageblade → Terminus / Wit’s End / Statikk | Tumble **35–65% → 50–80% AD**. Silver Bolts early %HP **2/5/8/11 → 6/7/8/9**. Statikk applies on-hit to bounce targets. Rageblade lost the “no crit” tax. Lethal Tempo now scales with *built* AS, which is her whole bag. |
| 8 | Overload surge carry | Zeri | Yun Tal / Stormrazor → Fiendhunter → IE | Overload duration **1.5→2.5s**, crit extension **4.5→7.5s**. Excess AS→AD **50→60%**. Fiendhunter is explicitly for her ult. |
| 9 | Frost-crit kiter | Ashe | Yun Tal / Stormrazor → IE → Hexoptics or RFC | Frost Shot bonus hit rewritten to scale with crit chance **and** (CritDamage−2). Slow **15–25% → 20–30%**. Ranger’s Focus mana 50→30. Hexoptics gives the range Magnetic Blaster used to. |
| 10 | Yun Tal stack-scaler / Jinx hyper | Jinx, Yunara, late Cait/Sivir | Yun Tal Wildarrows first → IE | New 3100 first item: 50 AD + 25% AS, **0% crit until you stack attacks to 25%**. Hypercarries who auto constantly get a cheaper IE setup than Stormrazor’s lane burst. Jinx R was tuned down; the *item* is the buff. |

**Close:** Miss Fortune (Double Up second hit = `60% × current crit damage`; Bullet Time extra-crit term) — Love Tap itself was **nerfed**. Kindred’s mark AD **15/25/35 → 30/40/50** is a jungle ADC story. Bloodthirster is now **75 AD + 15% lifesteal, no crit required** for all-in ADCs.

**Do not force:** Ezreal (Q damage and CD nerfed), Jhin (new 80% crit-damage modifier), Senna bot (mist crit 15%→10%, 90% crit hits, HP 600→570), Magnetic Blaster leftovers.

## Support

Enchanter items lost HP/AH and gained **heal/shield power**. Tank supports got **cheap mixed resists + team auras**. Mandate and Helia are new identities, not stat sticks.

| # | Playstyle | Best champs | Core spike | Why 7.3 |
|---|-----------|-------------|------------|---------|
| 1 | Mandate CC-mage | Nami, Lulu, Morgana, Seraphine, Zyra, Brand, Thresh | Bandleglass Mirror → **Imperial Mandate** | Mandate rebuilt: **60 AP**, **20 AH on CC abilities**, mark = **7% damage amp** for 4s. No more HP-gated, Nami-only proc. Every CC mage support’s first item. |
| 2 | Helia poke-into-heal | Karma, Nami, Sona, Seraphine, Lulu, Milio | Bandleglass → **Echoes of Helia** | New item. Stores **30% pre-mitigation** damage as fragments, dumps them as a heal when you shield/heal an ally. Poke supports finally convert damage into peel. |
| 3 | Ardent hypercarry leash | Lulu, Nami, Milio, Sona, Janna, Yuumi | **Ardent Censer** (2400) | 2700→**2400**, HSP 5%→**8%**, HP/AH stripped. Buff is a clean **30% AS + 25 on-hit magic** — no crit-chance math. Rides the entire ADC overhaul. |
| 4 | Harmonic chain-heal | Soraka, Sona, Nami, Seraphine, Milio | **Harmonic Echo** | Reworked: heal/shield **links 30%/35%** to the lowest-HP ally in range. Teamwide heal, not self-haste stacking. |
| 5 | Diadem mana-enchanter | Sona, Nami, Seraphine | Tear → **Whispering Circlet → Diadem of Songs** | HSP scales with **max mana**. At cap, heals the lowest-HP ally for **0.8% max mana/s** while in combat. Tear is 400g. |
| 6 | Abyssal aura tank | Leona, Nautilus, Alistar, Rell, Braum, Amumu | **Abyssal Mask** (2400) | 3000→**2400**, still a **12% magic-damage amp** aura. Low-economy tanks can actually finish it. Pairs with mage mid + Helia/Mandate bot. |
| 7 | Zeke delayed engage | Nautilus, Leona, Alistar, Rell, Thresh, Blitz | **Zeke’s Convergence** | Frostfire **waits up to 5s** if nobody is in range on ult cast. Hook/ult-from-fog tanks finally get the item. Mixed 25 armor / 25 MR. |
| 8 | Catcher AS-buff peel | Thresh, Rell, Leona, Nautilus, Rakan | **Yordle Trap** | Gold-snowball cut. Slow/CC now gives **you + nearby allies 30%/20% AS** (melee/ranged) and 20g on inspired kills. Direct ADC synergy. |
| 9 | Frozen Heart walk-in peel | Braum, Leona, Nautilus, Galio, Rammus | **Frozen Heart** | Aura is back to “stand in them”: **all enemies in 650 lose 25% AS**. No more “hit multiple people with abilities” tax. |
| 10 | Flowing Waters AP-haste | Karma, Lulu, Sona, Janna | **Staff of Flowing Waters** | 2500→2400, HSP 5%→**8%**, HP removed. 40 AP + 15 AH on the healee — the mage-comp twin of Ardent. |

**Close:** Salvation (cheaper, more HSP). Mantle of the Twelfth Hour (600 HP anti-burst, 2550g) on tank supports who get blown up by the new crit ADC.

**Do not force:** Senna support (mist/crit/HP all down). Old “stack HP + HSP + AH on one item” enchanter builds — Forbidden Idol is **HSP only** now.

## Mid

Mid’s 7.3 gifts are **two new champions**, **Legend: Haste (15 AH)**, **Luden’s single-target bias**, **Nashor as a real AP item**, and tanks losing Force of Nature’s **% magic damage reduction**.

| # | Playstyle | Best champs | Core spike | Why 7.3 |
|---|-----------|-------------|------------|---------|
| 1 | Palette control-mage | **Hwei** (new) | Blackfire or Luden → boots → Horizon / Orb / Deathcap | 10-spell kit (Disaster / Serenity / Torment + ult). The patch’s mid poster. Mandate is also real on him if you play CC-first. |
| 2 | Ult-steal AP fighter | **Sylas** (new) | Rocketbelt / Riftmaker → boots → Cosmic / Zhonya | Burst mage who copies the enemy ult. Conqueror AP ratio standardized to **1.667×**. Plays mid *or* jungle. |
| 3 | Luden’s isolated burst | Ahri, Syndra, Veigar, LeBlanc, Zoe, Orianna | **Luden’s Echo** → pen → Deathcap | Echo still hits the **primary** target for full damage; splash is the part that got cut. Single-target delete mages go up relative to waveclear bots. |
| 4 | DoT / battle mage vs tanks | Ryze, Cassiopeia, Vladimir, Swain, Taliyah, Hwei | Blackfire / Liandry → Demonic / Cosmic | Force of Nature **lost % magic DR**. Tanks cannot shrug DoT the same way. Smite-tank junglers will still be in lobbies — %HP burns matter more. |
| 5 | Legend: Haste weaver | Ryze, Cassio, Syndra, Orianna, Hwei, Ahri, Vladimir | Legend: Haste (replaces Tenacity) | Up to **15 ability haste** from combat. Tenacity is now a *boot* purchase, not a free rune. Spell-weavers win that trade. |
| 6 | Nashor AP on-hit | Kayle, Azir, Kennen, Teemo, Gwen, Cassio | **Nashor’s Tooth** (80 AP, 50% AS) | Adaptive Force gone. It is a dedicated AP on-hit item again (15 + 20% AP on-hit). |
| 7 | Essence Reaver AD caster | Lucian, Corki, (Ezreal only if you insist) | **Essence Reaver** → IE / Manamune | ER is **50 AD**, Sheen + crit, wider user pool. Lucian mid inherits the Culling-crit rewrite. Corki’s Q shifted toward AP; still an AD-caster item user. |
| 8 | Youmuu’s roam assassin | Talon, Zed, Qiyana, Pantheon, Naafiri | **Youmuu’s Ghostblade** | MS is now **always-on 4%** plus out-of-combat 30 (20 ranged). No more stack-to-move. Roam windows are the item. |
| 9 | HP-growth energy assassin | Akali, Katarina, Kassadin, Ekko | Protobelt / Stormsurge → pen | Akali HP/level **140→150**. Katarina HP **630→660**. Kassadin HP **630→650** + growth. They live long enough to use the new, fatter crit ADC as a meal — if they actually land. |
| 10 | Conqueror AP bruiser-mid | Sylas, Gwen, Kayle, Vladimir, Gragas | Conqueror + Riftmaker / Nashor | Conqueror AP per stack is **standardized 5–8.33**. No more 1.25–1.6× weirdness. |

**Do not force:** Yasuo / Yone mid (90% crit damage, excess-crit conversion 0.6→0.5 — they **anti-synergize** with the marksman patch). Ezreal mid (Q nerf). Infinity Orb users wait longer (crit threshold **35%→40%**).

## Jungle

Riot’s own text: tank and mage junglers should not need Sunfire to clear. Smite burn = `30–225 (+10% bAD +12% AP +20% bArmor +20% bMR +3% bonus HP)` true damage, two extra ticks after you stop hitting. Searing Crown is gone.

| # | Playstyle | Best champs | Core spike | Why 7.3 |
|---|-----------|-------------|------------|---------|
| 1 | Tank burn-clear / engage | Amumu, Rammus, Sejuani, Zac, Maokai, Nunu, Rell, Skarner | Bami’s → Sunfire **or skip it** → Frozen Heart / Twinguard / Abyssal / Mantle | Burn scales with **tank stats**. Twinguard is a tank capstone again (300 HP + 30% bonus resists at 5 stacks). Fighters are no longer supposed to steal it. |
| 2 | Mage jungle AP-clear | Fiddlesticks, Lillia, Karthus, Brand, Evelynn, Taliyah, Diana, Morgana | Liandry / Blackfire / Luden → boots → Rylai / Void | Smite **12% AP**. Monster penalties that used to kneecap ability junglers are being replaced by a burn that *likes* AP. Evelynn also gets HP 600→630. |
| 3 | Tunnel-gank fighter | **Rek’Sai** (new) | Warrior / Eclipse / Youmuu → Sterak / DD | New champion. Early ambush, knock-up, tunnel map control. Top *or* jungle. Sterak now has **20% tenacity baked in**. |
| 4 | Objective-setup tank | Jarvan, Amumu, Sejuani, Nautilus, Rek’Sai, Xin Zhao | Vision + tank mythic → Zeke / Abyssal / Radiant | Dragons/Baron/Herald have **real armor/MR**. Smite upgrades **600 → 1000 → 1400**. Burst steals lose; coordinated setup wins. |
| 5 | Protected full-clear farmer | Master Yi, Bel’Veth, Graves, Kindred, Kha’Zix, Shyvana | On-hit or crit jungle item → Kraken / IE / Rageblade | Laners get **50–80% XP** from camps. Jungle gold sits on **Smite stacks**. Farmers keep their camps. Lethal Tempo now wants the AS these champs buy. |
| 6 | Kindred mark-hunt ADC | Kindred | Kraken / Yun Tal → IE / Collector | Mark bonus AD **15/25/35 → 30/40/50**. She is a crit jungler in a crit patch. |
| 7 | Graves shotgun crit | Graves | Youmuu / Collector → IE / LDR | Shotgun crit multiplier **1.3 → 1.5** to match 200% crit. HP growth 128→136 (early HP down 660→630). |
| 8 | Cover-lane / catch-up jungler | Lee Sin, Xin Zhao, Pantheon, Jarvan, Nunu, Wukong | Early Warrior / Eclipse, then team items | Behind junglers get **70g** per Smite stack. Covering a crashing lane is less of a suicide XP-wise. Role is paid to be the teammate. |
| 9 | Zed / Yone monster-clear | Zed, Yone | Youmuu / Eclipse → lethality or crit | Zed **lost 80%/60% monster damage penalties** on Q/E. Yone Q vs monsters **65%→70%**. Both can actually jungle; Yone still has the 90% crit-damage cap. |
| 10 | On-hit Statikk fighter | Master Yi, Xin Zhao, Warwick, Lillia (on-hit), Viego | **Statikk Shiv** or Rageblade → BotRK / Terminus | Statikk chain-lightning **applies on-hit to bounce targets**. Yi HP/level 120→130. Warwick/Yi/Xin want Lethal Tempo’s new “scales with bonus AS” bullet. |

**Close:** Sylas jungle (same kit as mid). Viego (crit formulas updated to the new IE world, HP growth 128→138).

**Do not force:** Nunu *Snowball Barrage* 150% vs monsters **removed** — his tank Smite still helps, but that specific clear trick died. Searing Crown / Shimmering Spark builds. Laner-into-jungle smite-less Path of the raid boss (camps hurt and pay less).

## Top (Baron)

Melee champions deal **20% more turret damage**. Crystalline Overgrowth lets any champion chunk a tower with the first auto after crystals cook. Twinguard / Mantle / Frozen Heart / Sunfire were retargeted at **real tanks**. Death’s Dance and Trinity got **AD**. Tryndamere was rebuilt around **hitting hard**, not stacking AS.

| # | Playstyle | Best champs | Core spike | Why 7.3 |
|---|-----------|-------------|------------|---------|
| 1 | Heavy-crit split Trynd | Tryndamere | IE / Yun Tal / Stormrazor → Collector / BT / LDR | Passive **AS steroid deleted**. Base AD **54→64**. Bloodlust is missing-HP AD (up to **0.9 per 1%**). IE is 75 AD + 230% crit. Melee turret amp + Demolish + crystals. |
| 2 | Twinguard tank frontline | Ornn, Sion, Malphite, Cho’Gath, Maokai, Shen, Poppy, K’Sante | Sunfire / Hollow → **Amaranth’s Twinguard** | Twinguard: **300 HP**, 50/50 resists, **30% bonus armor/MR** at 5 combat stacks. Fighters were told to stop buying it. This is the tank IE. |
| 3 | Fimbulwinter mana-tank | Sion, Ornn, Shen, K’Sante, Poppy | Tear (400g) → **Fimbulwinter** (500 HP) | 15% max mana as HP, shield on CC. Tear items no longer eat mana to function. |
| 4 | Trinity Force skirmisher | Irelia, Jax, Camille, Fiora | **Trinity Force** (36 AD) → Sterak / DD / Wit’s | Trinity **30→36 AD**, Hearthbound Axe path, MS on the item gone (Phage still has Rage). These four also got **HP growth** (early HP trimmed). |
| 5 | Death’s Dance bruiser | Darius, Aatrox, Riven, Garen, Sett, Olaf, Renekton | **Death’s Dance** (50 AD, 45 armor) | DD was Defy-gated; now the **stats** are the item. 35→**50 AD**. Sterak’s **20% tenacity** is always on. |
| 6 | Crystal split-pusher | Fiora, Jax, Trynd, Yorick, Nasus, Camille, Sett | Trinity / Divine / IE + Demolish | First auto on a cooked turret deals **true %HP**. Plates never fall off. Melee **+20% turret damage**. Side-lane specialists finally get paid for touching a tower for 2 seconds. |
| 7 | Rek’Sai dive top | Rek’Sai | Eclipse / Youmuu → Sterak / DD | New early-game tunneler. Same kit as jungle, but the lane can use Crystalline Overgrowth and plate gold (plates persist, decay after 5:00). |
| 8 | Youmuu’s tempo fighter | Riven, Pantheon, Kled, Aatrox, Jayce, Quinn | **Youmuu’s** first or second | Persistent MS + out-of-combat MS, Spectral Haste stacks **removed**. Better for covering side waves and walking to Herald. |
| 9 | Legend: Haste ability fighter | Riven, Camille, Fiora, Aatrox, K’Sante, Gwen, Renekton | Legend: Haste | 15 AH from fighting. These kits are CD-limited, not tenacity-limited (Mercury’s Treads still exist). |
| 10 | Sunfire HP-tank | Sion, Cho’Gath, Dr. Mundo, Ornn, Malphite, Zac | **Sunfire Aegis** | Immolate is now **20 + 1.5% bonus HP**/s. No more attack-stack Flametouch. Giant HP tanks deal the damage; AS fighters do not. Mundo HP 720→680 early but growth exists; play for the HP pile. |

**Close:** Garen (HP 660→690, armor down — more of a DD/sterak bruiser). Frozen Heart / Mantle on tanks vs the new crit ADC. Olaf is mixed (early HP and Ragnarok resists down, HP growth 112→140).

**Do not force:** Fighter Twinguard. Force of Nature as a “I win vs AP” button (%DR gone). Tryndamere *attack-speed* Rageblade/Lethal-only builds — the kit was moved off that.

## How to use this on patch week

1. **Bot wins the patch.** If you can play Caitlyn, Sivir, Tristana, Xayah, or Twitch, do that. Pair with Mandate/Helia/Ardent, not Senna.
2. **Jungle is allowed to be a tank or a mage again.** Amumu/Rammus/Fiddle/Lillia are the actual 7.3 experiment, not another Hecarim clone.
3. **Top that touches towers** (Trynd, Fiora, Jax, tanks with crystals) is more valuable than top that only fights.
4. **Ban Rek’Sai or Sylas** if you do not want to learn them on day 1; Hwei is the harder mage and will be picked more than banned in lower ranks.
5. **Do not copy 7.2 Magnetic Blaster / Cloak of Agility / Nashor’s Talon / Searing Crown / Ingenious Hunter / Legend: Tenacity trees.** Those are gone.
