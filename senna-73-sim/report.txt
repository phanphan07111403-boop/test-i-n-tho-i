Why the live meta rushes Statikk Shiv
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  7.3 did not buff Senna's 1v1. It split Magnetic Blaster into three
  items: Hexoptics (range amp), Rapid Firecannon (energized range),
  Statikk Shiv (waveclear + on-hit bounce). Senna's pre-7.3 page WAS
  Magnetic. The meta is putting that kit back together, Shiv first,
  because Shiv is the only piece that crashes a wave.

  Official Shiv: Energized lightning bounces to 3/4/5/6 extras at
  1/5/9/13, 60 magic (90 vs minions), and COPIES ON-HIT onto bounce
  targets. Senna's Relic Cannon is 20% AD on-hit. Brutal is on-hit.
  Living Extraction is on-hit. Q applies on-hit to champions. One
  energized auto into a wave dumps Relic+90 mag through 4–6 minions.
  One energized auto into dragon copies Relic+extract onto the two
  people you did not click.

  AS ratio 0.4 is why she can rush it now. Old 0.125 ratio could not
  charge Energized. 30% AS actually arrives; Electrotherapy +5 per
  auto stacks it faster. Q refunds also speed the next proc.

  Support specifically: minions Senna does not last-hit spawn more
  wraiths. Shiv chips the wave, the ADC last-hits, souls pop. That
  is the fasting-Senna income the 1v1 table never scores.

  40 AP is live on this kit: Q heal 25% AP, R 70% AP. Youmuu has 0 AP.

Why 7.3 kept lethality first on ADC and moved support onto Hexoptics
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Mist crit 10%/20. At 8:00 an ADC has ~40 souls = 10% crit. Hexoptics'
    25% crit is real, but Youmuu's 55 AD + 15 lethality + 15 AH punches
    a squishy harder while souls are still low. Caitlyn rushes Hex
    because Headshot scales with crit immediately. Senna does not.
  • Collector 2nd is the 12:00 fill: 25% crit + 12 leth + execute,
    3000g, so you are not sitting on Youmuu waiting for a 3500g IE.
  • Hexoptics 3rd is the Magnetic replacement — 55 AD, 25% crit, +9–10%
    at the range Senna already lives at. Completes ~16:00 on ADC gold.
  • IE is 4th/5th. 3500g is a hole at 16:00 (Youmuu+Col+Hex is ~8900g
    of the 10180; IE needs 3500 more). It lands with LDR at ~24:00.
  • Autos crit for 90% of 200% = 180% (207% with IE). IE is still the
    late spike, just not the opener.
  • AS ratio 0.4 means Berserkers/RFC/Fiendhunter actually convert.
    They still lose 8:00 and 12:00 to lethality because Statikk/Yun Tal
    /Stormrazor buy 0–25% crit and little lethality while mist is empty.
    RFC is a 4th-item range card, not a first.
  • Yun Tal starts at 0% crit and needs 125 autos. Senna already
    builds crit from mist — do not stack a second delayed engine.
  • Draktharr is the dive Youmuu. Nightstalker is one proc; Youmuu
    keeps 15 AH + Spectral Haste for the AA→Q kite. Poke Senna
    wants Youmuu. All-in DH Senna can still Drak.
  • Support cannot buy Youmuu at 8:00. Sickle gold finishes Hexoptics
    ~11:00. Second legendary is Mortal (pen + grievous) or Essence
    Reaver (Q spellblade + 20 AH) — they tie on 20:00 gold. RFC 2nd
    delays AD/pen and loses. Statikk 1st has 0% crit and loses.

Traps
  • Magnetic Blaster / Cloak of Agility — deleted.
  • Hexoptics first on ADC, copying Caitlyn. Youmuu first is +6–7%
    on the 24-min area. Hex is 3rd, after Collector.
  • Yun Tal / Stormrazor first — delayed or expensive crit, no leth.
  • Statikk → RFC as the WRF page. Lightning is waveclear/clump.
    1v1 poke wants AD + lethality + Hex, not 0% crit Shiv.
  • IE second — 3500g hole. Collector 3000g fills 12:00.
  • Support finishing IE/LDR — 20-min sickle gold completes two
    legendaries. Hex then Mortal/ER. Third is a maybe.
  • Enchanter (Ardent → Helia → Harmonic) is a different question;
    that sim already picked Mandate 4th. This file is damage Senna.
  • Gathering Storm / Ingenious Hunter / Legend: Tenacity — illegal or
    wrong identity. Legend: Haste is fine if you skip Transcendence.

Swaps
  • ADC Hex 2nd (Youmuu → Hex, skip Collector) if you need the range
    amp before 12:00. -2.8% area, Hex lands at 12 instead of Collector.
  • Draktharr first if you are the DH dive Senna, not the poke Senna.
  • Mortal Reminder over LDR vs heal comps. Support already prefers it.
  • Essence Reaver 2nd on support is a coin-flip with Mortal. Take ER
    when the lane is Q-heal, Mortal when they have a healer.
  • Serpent's Fang 3rd vs Lulu/Karma/Janna/sett shields.
  • Shieldbow or GA 6th vs burst. Mercurial vs Malz/WW.
  • Steelcaps into all-in AD supports (Leona/Naut/Rell). -5% poke.
  • Ionian on support is -0.1% vs Berserkers+Hex+ER. Take it when
    Q/Flash cadence matters more than the auto (enchanter split).
  • AP carry lane partner: this is still an AD Senna page. Do not
    force Ardent on a damage page — that is the enchanter file.
