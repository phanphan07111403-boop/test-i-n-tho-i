WHAT 7.3 CHANGED
  • Base crit 175% → 200%, IE capstone 230% / 75 AD
  • Magnetic Blaster removed — power split into Stormrazor / RFC / Shiv
  • Shiv is on-hit bounce (40 AD / 40 AP / 30% AS)
  • Rageblade: 35 AD / 30 AP, no longer blocks crit
  • Nashor: dedicated 80 AP on-hit again
  • New: Yun Tal, Fiendhunter Bolts, Hexoptics C44, Stormrazor
  • Kits: Twitch stealth rework, Vayne/Caitlyn/Lucian/Xayah/Trist/Zeri crit hooks

VERDICT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Play 7.3 as one of these, in order of who gained the most:
    1) Kalista Rend-Shiv — on-hit spear dump
    2) Teemo Nashor-Shiv — ap on-hit poison
    3) Caitlyn trap-Headshot — crit sniper
    4) Varus blight-Shiv — on-hit artillery
    5) Twitch stealth-Fiendhunter — stealth ult burst
    6) Lucian Culling-crit — crit tempo
    7) Zeri Fiendhunter-R — ult-window carry
    8) Ashe Focus-crit — crit utility kiter
    9) Kayle Nashor-ascend — ap on-hit scaler
    10) Kai'Sa hybrid on-hit — hybrid on-hit

  Three lanes of 7.3, don't mix them:
  • ON-HIT CLUMP  (Shiv → Rageblade → Terminus/Nashor)
      Varus / Kog'Maw / Kalista / Teemo
  • CRIT SNIPER   (Stormrazor or Yun Tal → IE → RFC/Fiendhunter)
      Caitlyn / Lucian / Xayah / Jinx / Tristana / Twitch / Zeri
  • DUELIST       (Kraken → Rageblade → Terminus) — not Shiv
      Vayne

  Traps:
  • Magnetic Blaster is gone. Crit ADCs who still buy 'the waveclear item'
    want Stormrazor (lane) or RFC (range), not Shiv.
  • Vayne Silver Bolts do not bounce. Shiv is a bait.
  • Jhin / Senna / Yasuo / Yone were compensated — they are not 7.3 winners.
  • Ezreal Q was nerfed; Muramana QoL does not cover it.
  • Kog'Maw Shiv is a late teamfight upgrade — 7.2 BotRK wins 12–16.
  • Vayne Tumble/R went up, rank-4 Silver Bolts 11% → 9%. Lane up, 3-item flat.
