  IS DARK HARVEST WORTH IT ON Q-SPAM ZIGGS?
  No as the default. Yes only if you are already snowballing squishies.

  WHY HARVEST LOSES THE SPAM PATTERN:
  • Harvest needs the target below 50% HP, then 35s CD (1s on takedown).
    Q-spam's job is chunking people who start the window at full HP.
    At 16:00 a full-HP squishy: DH proc chance 0% — mean path never crosses 50% in 12s.
  • Comet doesn't care about HP. Long bombs also travel far, so the
    V26.09 distance amp is real on bounce-Q. It ticks every ~8–12s.
    Same Luden+SF items @22 mix: Comet 864 vs DH 805 (-7%).
  • Short Fuse is proc damage — it does NOT harvest. Stay at 1400 and
    only the bomb itself can proc DH.
  • Live data still locks Comet (Manaflow / Transcendence / Scorch).

  WHEN HARVEST IS THE PAGE:
  • Not on the first poke. Comet's long-travel bomb (V26.09 distance
    amp) is a bigger proc than Harvest at average souls, and it repeats.
    Execute @22 same items: Comet 980 vs DH 985 (+0%).
  • Snowball + takedown reset (second harvest in the 12s window)
    @22 mix: DH snow 861 vs Comet 864 (-0%), souls 17.
    That's a fed fight with a kill in the window — not Q-spam lane.
  • Item for DH: Shadowflame (Cinderbloom <40%). Item for Comet poke:
    Horizon (10% on every bomb after the first) or Liandry vs HP.

  HOW TO PLAY Q-SPAM ZIGGS:
  1) Runes: Arcane Comet, Manaflow, Transcendence, Scorch.
     Precision: Presence of Mind + Cut Down / Coup de Grace.
  2) Items: Luden's Echo → Sorcs → Shadowflame → Deathcap
     (Horizon / Void if they stack HP/MR; Liandry vs tanks).
  3) Max Q. Throw bounce bombs on CD from 1400. Don't walk up
     for Short Fuse unless they already can't punish.
  4) Take Dark Harvest only into 4+ squishies when you will get
     takedown resets. Then rush Luden + Shadowflame and look for
     the bomb that lands below 50%, not the one that starts the poke.
