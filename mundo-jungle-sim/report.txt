VERDICT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Best jungle path: HS → Warmog → Thorn

  CLEAR — the burn replaced Sunfire, it did not replace Heartsteel.
  • 4:00 AoE camps: belt components 38s, Bami's 36s (Bami saves 1.5s). Single-target camps are 28s vs 28s.
  • Same items with the burn turned off: belt 66s, Bami 51s (Bami saves 14s). That is the old reason to buy immolate.
  • Burn damage on the 4:00 clear is 5822 true from belt components and 5253 from Bami's. It ignores the camps' 35 MR. Immolate on that clear is 1232.
  • Under a 1-second burn tick, Bami's saves 1.5s on AoE camps. That is not a reason to delay Heartsteel.
  • Every tested path finishes the rotation above ~85% HP. The clear is not
    the filter anymore. The dragon fight is.

  FIRST DRAGON (~8:00)
  • Heartsteel online ~7:00. Fight 1642 dmg, lives 11.2s, 2311 HP.
  • Bami → Heartsteel delays Heartsteel to ~11:00. At 8:00: 1603 dmg, lives 11.0s, 2309 HP.
  • Sunfire first finishes Sunfire ~9:00 and Heartsteel ~16:00. At 8:00: 1512 dmg, lives 9.8s, 2009 HP.
  • Sunfire first is behind the Heartsteel rush by 130 damage and 1.5s of survival at the first dragon.

  SECOND ITEM (~16:00)
  • Warmog ~16:00: 2441 dmg, lives 22.2s, 4098 HP, 470 Heartsteel stacks.
  • Mantle ~15:00: 2408 dmg, lives 22.0s, 3995 HP, 467 stacks.
  • Sunfire second (after Bami → HS): 2520 dmg, lives 19.0s, 3678 HP, 325 stacks.
  • Sunfire first, Heartsteel late: 2418 dmg, lives 17.8s, 3419 HP, only 66 stacks.
  • Best second legendary after a Heartsteel rush: Warmog's Armor.
    Warmog vs Mantle at 16:00 is +33 damage and +0.2s. Same spike. Warmog wins the long
    fight; Mantle is the cheaper buy when one burst is what kills you.

  WHY
  • Patch 7.3 Smite burn: 30–225 by level + 10% bonus AD + 20% bonus armor
    + 20% bonus MR + 3% bonus HP true damage. This sim ticks it once per
    second on the monster being autoed (the notes say the hit repeats,
    and up to two more times after you stop; they do not print the interval).
  • Giant's Belt already feeds that burn. Sunfire's new immolate is
    20 + 1.5% bonus HP magic per second, at 130% vs monsters, into 35 MR,
    with Flametouch and the stack amp removed.
  • Heartsteel is 700 HP, a 140 + 3.5% max HP proc, and permanent HP.
    E, W, and R all read bonus HP. So does the burn. The Sunfire-first
    path is still on components at 8:00 and finishes Heartsteel ~16:00,
    so the stacks stay behind (66 vs 470 HP).
  • Warmog's Armor (7.0) is the old Spirit Visage button: +30% healing
    and regen, plus 3.5% max HP/s after 5s out of combat if you have
    950 bonus HP. Mundo's passive, W, and R all pick up the amp.
  • Mantle of the Twelfth Hour is 2550g, 600 HP, and a lifeline under
    30% HP. Buy it when one burst rotation is the way you die.
    Buy Sunfire second only when the fights are stacked melees and the
    immolate is hitting more than one champion.

  RUNES
  Keystone: Grasp of the Undying.
  Resolve: Unshakeable, Second Wind, Overgrowth.
  Precision: Last Stand.
  Spells: Flash + Smite.
  Grasp and Overgrowth are the HP runes Heartsteel, E, W, R, and the
  Smite burn all read. Second Wind is the clear sustain. Last Stand
  matches E, which already hits harder when you are missing health.
  Swap Second Wind for Nullifying Orb into heavy magic burst.
  Swap Last Stand for Legend: Haste if you want more Q and W uptime.

  BUY ORDER
  1. Ruby Crystal.
  2. Giant's Belt → Kindlegem → Heartsteel. Do not stop for Bami's.
     Heartsteel is up ~7:00. Proc it on scuttle and the first dragon.
  3. Plated Steelcaps. Armored Advance after 10:00 only if it does not
     delay the next legendary.
  4. Warmog's Armor. The heal amp is the second spike.
     Mantle of the Twelfth Hour instead if they can burst you under 30%.
     Sunfire Aegis instead if you are fighting inside a pile of melees.
  5. Thornmail if they heal off autos. Otherwise Amaranth's Twinguard.
  Skill order: Q, E, Q, W, then max Q → E → W. R at 6 / 11 / 15.
  Q is the monster cap. E is the HP ratio. W is the heal.

  Trap: finishing Sunfire before Heartsteel. The clear barely changes and
  the 8:00 fight loses 130 damage and 1.5s of uptime.
  Trap: buying Bami's "for the clear." It saves 1.5s of AoE camps and pushes Heartsteel from ~7:00 to ~11:00.
