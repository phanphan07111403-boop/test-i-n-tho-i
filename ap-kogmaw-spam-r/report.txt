AP KOG'MAW — SPAM R  (PC LoL patch 26.19, Living Artillery from 26.16)
Playstyle: Q shred, then R on cooldown. No W, no E. Window: 12s fight.
R mana: 40 per shell + 40 per stack, cap 400. Stacks fall off after 8s.
Score: 55% half-mana barrage (you were already spamming) + 45% full-mana siege.
Inside a barrage: 62% tank / 38% squishy. Damage stops at the target's HP.
The target stands in the shells. No heal, no shield, no sidestep. Real games are lower; the ranking is what survives that.
Runes: Comet, Manaflow, Transcendence, Gathering Storm, Presence of Mind.

  then let the 8s stack timer fall and fire the cheap shells again.

  • Fastest pattern: 4 shells, then reset (kill 11.7s, 8 shells total).
  • Dumping until the shell costs 400 kills at 12.3s and leaves 53 mana. It is not faster.
  • Costs: 40, 80, 120, 160, 200, 240, 280, 320, 360, 400.
    Shot 1 is ~3–5× the damage per mana of shot 6. Raw damage barely moves
    until the target is under 40% HP, where the shell doubles.
  • On a half bar the chain dies, the stacks fall off, and the next
    shells are cheap again. That second volley is the spam. Don't hold
    the button through 300 mana waiting for it.
  • Q first. Rank 5 shreds 32% resistances for 4 seconds. Spam R maxes
    Q, not W. W is the other build (hide and auto).

  WHY THIS SHAPE
  • Finished builds all kill the squishy in ~2–3s. The tank is the choice.
  • Malignance first (~7:00): 20 ultimate haste, 600 mana, Hatefog
    (60 + 5% AP per second, refreshed by the next R, 10 MR shred).
  • Horizon second beats Liandry second on a half bar at 22:00: 3663 vs 3186.
    Horizon is +25 AH (another shell) and 10% damage on the long-range hit.
    Liandry is 2% max HP per second. The chain does not last long enough
    for the burn to pass the extra shell plus the amp. Buy Liandry when
    fights are long and the tank is healing; this pattern is not that.
  • Tear into Seraph has the mana (2815) and the shells (11 on a full bar) and still loses the half-mana tank (2265).
    The extra shells show up late, without Void. Pen on 8 shells beats
    a 9th and 10th shell into uncut MR.
  • Actualizer is a trap here (2392 half-tank). The active doubles costs for 8s.
    R's cost is already climbing. Leave the active unpressed and the
    item is just a small Lost Chapter legendary.
  • Luden into Malignance is the close second (3317 half-tank). Echo is one proc.
    The second Lost Chapter item delays Void. One mana item is enough.
  • Ionian Boots are 10 AH. 12 magic pen from Sorcs is the boot.
