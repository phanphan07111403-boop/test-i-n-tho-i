Patch 7.3. Solo lane. Goal: strongest late-game spike on a finished page.

STACK RULE
Dusk and Dawn Spellblade: after an ability, the next attack applies
on-hit effects again (1.5s cooldown). That second application is a stack.
E-A = 2 stacks on Empowerment and 2 stacks on Kraken.
Empowerment procs at 3 (60–200 adaptive + 9% damage for the rest of the fight).
Kraken procs at 3 (120–168 physical ranged, up to +75% from missing health).
Guinsoo Phantom Hit is a separate extra on-hit and adds a stack the same way.

WHY THIS PAGE
Dusk makes E-A worth two stacks, so Empowerment and Kraken proc on the
next auto. Guinsoo is the first buy after that because Phantom Hit is
another extra on-hit: same stacks, and it repeats E passive, Nashor, and
Terminus. Terminus is the armor pen for Kraken plus magic pen for the
on-hits, stacking to 30% of each. Nashor is the late spike — its on-hit
is the one both doubles keep firing, and it is the biggest completion
jump on the curve.
Deathcap sets sit behind this. The winning page has a modest AP pool,
and the cap does not add an on-hit for the extra stack to repeat.
Void does not replace Terminus here. Void is magic pen only, and item
percent pen caps at 40%, so stacking it on Terminus wastes the overlap.
Boots of Mana into Spellslinger beat Berserker into Gunmetal. The full
page is already at the 2.50 attack speed cap, so more attack speed does
not add swings. Spellslinger's penetration does.
Lich Bane is out. It is the same Spellblade unique as Dusk.

ASSUMPTIONS
- 6s all-in. Damage keeps counting after the first target drops, so two
  pages are not tied just because both delete one body. Q and E open
  together (20% shred for 4s). The first auto is E-A. W primes Dusk when
  Q and E are both down. R is off in the ranking fight and on in the combo line.
- A minute with no new item can dip. The target's resists are still climbing.
- Extra on-hit repeats E passive, Nashor, Brutal, Guinsoo, Terminus, and
  adds Empowerment / Kraken stacks. It does not repeat the auto, the wave,
  Dusk's own Spellblade damage, or E's missing-health active.
- Waves (level 9, Exalted) hit the primary once per attack for E passive damage.
- Kayle is ranged from level 5, so Kraken uses the ranged numbers after that.
- Empowerment and Brutal are magic once AP is at least bonus AD.
- Terminus Dark stacks 10% armor and magic pen, up to 30%. Item percent pen
  is capped at 40%, so Void + Terminus does not go past 40% magic pen.
- Attack speed cap 2.50. Zeal is permanent at level 13.
- Rune page in the item curve is the kill-score winner above.
  A legal page is one keystone, three slots from the primary path, and
  one rune from a second path. Bone Plating is the usual defensive
  flex and is not in the damage.
- Gathering Storm AP totals from 6:00, every 3 minutes: 4, 10, 18, 28,
  then onward. Absolute Focus is 4–40 AP while Kayle stays above 65% HP.
- Lethal Tempo is the 7.3 bullet: 6.4% attack speed per stack for ranged,
  and at 6 stacks a 6–24 adaptive bullet that scales with bonus attack speed.
  It does not break the 2.50 cap. Conqueror is 5–8.33 AP per stack at
  the AP adaptive, 6 stacks, one stack per cast or attack.
- Boot rush is tier-2 (Berserker or Mana). The matching upgrade
  (Gunmetal or Spellslinger) finishes that same slot once the legendaries
  are in. The search picks the line with the higher full-page score.
- Enchant is not modeled. It does not change the damage page.
