VERDICT
  Take the damage build in a 1v1 and in a teamfight. Full haste casts
  more often, but each Flux Overload is about half as large, and a 4s
  fight only fits one or two extra spells before cast time gets in the way.
  Top 1 kills the squishy in 0.75s, the bruiser in 1.67s, the tank in 3.17s.
  Full AH takes 1.93s, 3.64s, and 5.87s.
  Over 4s on a clump that does not die: Top 1 16886, Full AH 12584 (75%).
  The balance point is not 140 ability haste. Swapping Rod of Ages for
  Blackfire Torch (Seraph, Deathcap, Infinity Orb, Void, Spellslinger)
  is 45 item AH and the highest damage in the search:
  4s clump 20664, team dead at 3.76s.
  Past that, more haste means selling Deathcap, Void, or Orb, and the
  kill times get worse. Rod of Ages stays correct if you need its 550 HP;
  in that case drop Infinity Orb, not Void Staff or Deathcap.

SHARED RUNES (every build)
  Phase Rush, Manaflow (300 mana), Transcendence (+10 AH, 8% refund / 8s),
  Legend: Haste (15 AH). Nimbus is move speed only, not in the damage.
  Phase Rush: after 3 hits, basic cooldowns cut 20% and +10 AH for 3s.

ASSUMPTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Arcane Mastery: max mana = (base + items + Manaflow) × (1 + AP/1000).
  Seraph Awe is 2% max mana as AP (current wiki), refunds 25% mana, no magic pen.
  Deathcap is +30% AP and multiplies Awe, Riftmaker HP-to-AP, and Blackfire's
  4% AP per burning champion. Blackfire burn is 20 + 2% AP per second.
  Liandry burn is 2% max HP over 3 seconds (about 0.67% HP per second) plus
  Madness, +2% damage per second up to +6%. Riftmaker is +2% per second up to +8%,
  and +2% bonus HP as AP.
  Infinity Orb is +20% ability damage when the target is already under 40% HP.
  Luden echo every 9s. Rocketbelt once: 100 + 15% AP, 1.2× on a lone target.
  Bloodletter shreds 7.5% MR per hit, up to 30%. It cannot be paired with Void
  or Cryptbloom. Percent pen from RoA, Void, and Spellslinger multiplies.
  Malignance Hatefog is not counted: Realm Warp deals no damage.
  Crimson/Ionian Summoned lowers summoner spells, not Q/W/E.
  Horizon's 10% amp needs a cast from 600 range. EQ is inside 550, so it is
  not applied. The item is still in the pool as 80 AP and 25 AH.
  You are free-casting. Stuns, Zhonya, and missed Qs are not modeled.
  Move speed from Cosmic, Phase Rush, and Spell Flux is not damage, but it is
  why a haste build gets to spend the full 8 seconds in range.
  Cast time caps an EQ loop at 0.50s. None of these builds reach that cap.
