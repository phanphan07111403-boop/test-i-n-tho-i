ASSUMPTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Phase Rush is on every page. Its 10 haste and 20% cooldown cut still happen.
  Gathering Storm is 40 AP from 18:00 until 21:00, then 54 AP. Deathcap,
  Seraph, and Arcane Mastery all see that AP.
  Absolute Focus is 40 AP at level 15 and is included only while Ryze is
  above 65% health. The damage fights do not hit Ryze, so it stays on there.
  Scorch is 49 magic damage every 8 seconds, one champion, then magic resist.
  Battle Zeal is 1.4% per second on basic abilities, up to 4.2% at 3 seconds.
  It does nothing to a kill that ends at 0.75s.
  Cut Down is 6.5% damage against champions above 60% health.
  Coup de Grace is 8% damage against champions below 40% health.
  Last Stand is 5% at full health up to 11% at 30% health.
  Legend: Haste is 15. Legend: Bloodline is 7% omnivamp, full value on a
  one-champion fight. Area spells against a team heal at 33% of that.
  The burst is three hits of 1,000 post-mitigation magic damage.
  Bone Plating shaves 60 off each of those hits. Seraph's shield is 16% of
  current mana when a hit would cross 35% health. Nullifying Orb is 180.
  Courage of the Colossus is 45 + 1% max health, from landing Rune Prison.
  Overgrowth is 50 stacks: 150 health, then +3% health.
  Second Wind, for a ranged champion, is 5 health per 5 seconds plus
  6 + 2% missing health over 5 seconds after you are hit.
  Revitalize is +5% to your heals and shields, or +15% under 40% health.
  It does not increase omnivamp. Nimbus Cloak and Ixtali Seedjar do not
  change these numbers. Axiom Arcanist does not either: Realm Warp deals
  no damage. Brutal and Legend: Alacrity are auto-attack runes.
