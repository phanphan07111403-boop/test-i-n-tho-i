ASSUMPTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Conqueror at level 15 is 8.33 AP per stack, 50 AP at 6 stacks, before
  Deathcap. One stack per spell cast. The spell deals damage with the
  stacks it was cast on, then the new stack can heal that hit. Stacks
  fall off after 6 seconds without a hit. Ryze's combo reaches 6 stacks
  on the sixth cast, which is after the squishy is already dead.
  Taking Conqueror makes Precision the main tree. The other three runes
  here are Battle Zeal, Cut Down, and Legend: Haste, plus one Sorcery rune.
  Manaflow, Transcendence, and Gathering Storm cannot all stay.
  Legend: Bloodline is 7% omnivamp from the first point of damage.
  Omnivamp heals the full amount of champion damage, including area
  spells and the Blackfire burn. It does not heal more against minions.
  Riftmaker is the 7.2 version: 70 AP, 15 haste, 350 health, 11% magic
  vamp, and 2% of bonus health as AP. Void Corruption still ramps to 8%
  damage and, at 4 seconds, adds 6% omnivamp because Ryze is ranged.
  Magic vamp is the spellvamp stat. Area damage heals at one third:
  Flux Overload, the Spell Flux spread, and the burn. The first Spell
  Flux hit and a raw Overload heal at the full 11%.
  The shared item table still lists Riftmaker at 80 AP. This run uses 70.
  Hextech Gunblade was removed. Gluttonous Greaves are a component boot,
  not a sixth item. Blade of the Ruined King is omnivamp on an attack
  item, so it is not a spellvamp source for this build. Triumph heals
  on a takedown. It is not vamp.
