  IS SPEAR-ONLY Q SPAM WORTH PLAYING?
  No — not as Nidalee's identity. Yes — as the setup / siege phase.

  WHY SPEAR-ONLY LOSES THE CHAMPION:
  • Hunt exists to buff Takedown (+30%) and Pounce range. If you
    never convert, you spent the kit's payoff button on a mark
    you refuse to cash.
  • A landed max spear is a chunk, not a kill. At 16:00 the spear
    is 458 into a 2280 HP squishy (20% HP).
    The convert combo is 946 (41% HP) — that's a pick
    once they're already tagged, or with ignite / a teammate.
  • Expected max-range accuracy is ~32% vs mobile targets. Even a
    charitable mix (70% mid-range / 30% max) still loses the
    squishy window because you never cash Hunt. Convert uses a
    short Hunt spear (~68% hit) then melee that almost always lands.
  • Lich Bane (~91% pick, her actual core) is Spellblade
    on the Takedown auto. Spear-only never attacks, so the item is
    dead. Same Lich path @22 squish: SO 713 vs CV 1329.
  • Jungle clear / scuttle / 2v2 still needs cougar QWE. Spear-only
    as a full-game plan is a different (worse) champion.

  WHEN Q SPAM *IS* THE CORRECT BUTTON:
  • Pre-objective poke and vs tanks you should not melee.
    Liandry+Horizon spear-only vs tank @22: 840 vs Lich-convert 519 (+62%).
    Convert onto a healthy tank is discounted (55% commit) because
    you often just die. Spear-only is the tank pattern.
  • Horizon Focus 10% is real on long spears AND on the convert
    that follows a ≥600-range Hunt mark (6s). Don't skip cougar
    just because you bought a poke item.

  HOW TO PLAY IT:
  1) Clear and fight cougar. Human is for spears / heal / traps.
  2) Spam Q to apply Hunt, not to farm poke damage. Short spears
     that land beat max-range spears that miss.
  3) Convert: Pounce (Hunt range) → Swipe → Takedown+Lich.
  4) Vs tanks / siege: stay human, Liandry ticks, don't dive.
  5) Build: Lich Bane → Sorcs → Rocketbelt → Shadowflame/Zhonya
     into squishies. Swap Liandry/Horizon/Void when they stack HP.
     Do not rush Lich if you refuse to auto.
