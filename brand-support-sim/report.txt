8-second fight, ultimate up. One frontline + one carry. No runes.

HOW THE ITEMS INTERACT ON BRAND
  Brand has no heal or shield. Helia, Harmonic and Ardent do nothing
  until something heals or shields an ally.
  Diadem is that trigger: while Brand is in combat it heals the lowest
  ally for 0.8% max mana per second, and that heal spends Helia shards.
  Helia stores 30% of pre-mitigation champion damage, capped at 80–250
  per spend. Diadem spends every second, so the cap is per second.
  Before Diadem, shards sit in the item and expire as unused healing.
  Font of Life would spend them on the Q stun. It is not in this run.
  Harmony on 7.3b is the 7.3a nerf: +0.25% of max mana as heal/shield power.

VERDICT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Healing: this Brand build does not replace a healer.
  At 10:00 Brand's fight heal is 0 (no Diadem yet) against Soraka's 1574.
  At 14:00 Helia is finished and Brand still heals 0.
  Shards have nowhere to go until Diadem, or Mikael's, actually heals an ally.
  At 22:00 on the gold clock, Brand restores 1638 HP in the 8s fight
  (Soraka 3788, Nami 1358, Yuumi 1410, Sona heal 975 + shield 879,
  Seraphine heal 1086 + shield 1212).
  That is 43% of Soraka. Most of Brand's number is Helia
  turning the damage he just dealt into healing, and it stops the moment he stops hitting.
  On raw fight heal he is 121% of Nami and near Yuumi.
  Count shields and he falls behind Seraphine, Sona and Yuumi. He has no shield of his own.
  Lane heal/min at 22:00, ultimates excluded: Brand 1554, Soraka 11617, Nami 3475.
  Lane assumes heals are cast on cooldown about 70% of the minute. Soraka's regen at
  six items can actually pay for that. Brand's lane heal is only the Diadem ticks
  during trades, plus Helia on W poke.
  Sona's mana growth is 4.9, so her Diadem tick is the small one. Her shield is the kit.

  Ardent vs Mikael's on the full 6-item sheet (level 13, gold ignored):
  Ardent: heal 1564, damage 1995, 198 AP.
  Mikael: heal 1766, damage 1852, 148 AP.
  Mikael's fight heal is higher by 202, because the active lands in this fight.
  That active is on a 75s cooldown. Ardent's 50 AP and 8% heal/shield power are up every fight,
  and Ardent deals 143 more damage.
  Buy Mikael's for the cleanse. Buy Ardent if the carry auto-attacks and you want the higher damage.
  On the 22-minute clock Ardent only just finishes, and Mikael's is ~40 gold short.
  The 6th item is not what decides the game. Diadem is.

  Damage: the enchanter path gives up the reason to pick Brand.
  At 22:00, Diadem Brand deals 2068.
  Liandry → Rylai → Mandate deals 3280.
  Blackfire → Liandry → Rylai deals 3270.
  Liandry Brand is 59% more damage on this fight.
  Same minute: Zyra 3780, Morgana 2135, Lux 2150.
  Liandry is the gap: 2% max HP per second on the frontline, refreshed by Blaze,
  plus 7% magic penetration. Helia's 40 AP does not replace that.
  Full sheet, same targets: Ardent Brand 1995, Liandry Brand 2946, Zyra 3691.

  What this build is:
  From 0:00 to the Diadem minute, a mage support with no Liandry and no heal.
  After Diadem, a combat heal that can pass Nami's fight total and still
  sit at about 40% of Soraka, with no shield and with lane healing well behind.
  Damage lands next to Morgana and Lux and well behind Liandry Brand and Zyra.
  Harmonic Echo only copies a heal. Brand does not have one until Diadem.
  Pick Liandry → Rylai when Brand is in the game to deal damage.
  Pick Soraka, Nami, Yuumi or Seraphine when the slot has to heal.
