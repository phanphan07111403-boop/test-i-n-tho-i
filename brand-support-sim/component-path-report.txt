WHAT WAS OPTIMIZED
  Spend gold on the next affordable piece. Do not sit on gold to finish
  a legendary in one buy. Helia stores 30% of pre-mitigation damage and
  spends all of it only when something heals an ally. Brand and Zyra have
  no heal, so that something is Diadem. The conversion minute is the first
  minute both Diadem and Helia are owned.
  Circlet charges 8 procs a minute at 10 AH, up to the 12/min cap:
  rate = min(12, 8 × (100 + AH) / 110). The buy minute adds 0 charges.
  50 charges transform Circlet into Diadem. Harmony is +0.25% of max mana
  as heal/shield power.

  Orders checked: 30240.
  Conversion, this order: 13:00 (Circlet 7:00, Helia 13:00, Diadem 13:00).
  Conversion, Liandry third: 21:00 (Circlet 15:00, Helia 19:00, Diadem 21:00).
  All 5 legendaries: this order 22:00, Liandry third 23:00.
  Gold left in pocket at the end of each minute, 0:00 through 23:00: this order 4620, Liandry third 8420.

WHY THE DAMAGE BECOMES A HEAL HERE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  At 13:00 Helia is bought and Circlet becomes Diadem on the same minute.
  Charges that minute: 50.9 (need 50).
  Zyra 8s fight: 1964 damage, 1030 heal.
    Diadem's own heal 141. Helia turning damage into heal 889.
  Brand 8s fight: 1583 damage, 944 heal (Helia portion 802).
  Helia stores 30% of pre-mitigation damage and spends it only when Diadem
  heals an ally. The heal before that minute is 0 even if Helia is owned.

  Circlet is finished at 7:00. Minute 6 has 2700 gold, short of Sickle plus Circlet (2900).
  The buy minute adds 0 charges. Six minutes at 10 AH are 48 charges, short of 50,
  so the clock needs haste above the Scythe's 10 before it can land on 13:00.
  No checked order converts before 13:00. This one buys Helia's
  Kindlegem at 11:00, the first minute its 1000 gold is up after Ashes.
  Fated Ashes is finished at 9:00 with the gold in front of that
  Kindlegem. Moving Ashes later does not finish Liandry sooner: Sickle + Circlet
  + Helia + Liandry is 8300 gold, and 16:00 has 7900. Liandry is 17:00.
  Harmonic Echo is 22:00. It multiplies a heal, so it is last
  among the legendaries. Its mirror is combined on the Harmonic minute, so the
  Tome's 20 AP stays until that buy replaces it.
  Ionian boots finish at 23:00. Those pieces are bought only
  when they do not push Helia, Liandry, or Harmonic to a later minute.
