SAME ITEMS, SO THE KIT IS THE WHOLE GAP
  Neither champion heals or shields. Helia stores 30% of pre-mitigation
  champion damage and spends it when Diadem heals an ally.
  In a short fight both fill that cap, so fight healing ties.
  Over a minute of lane, the champion who keeps Liandry ticking heals more.
  Harmony is the 7.3b rate: +0.25% of max mana as heal/shield power.

UTILITY DETAIL AT 20:00
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Brand stun: 1.75s, only if the target is already ablaze. Expected
  value uses E hit 90% then Q hit 72%. One target. Mikael's cleanses it.
  Brand also has a point-click E, so the blaze setup does not miss as a skillshot.
  Zyra root: 1.0–1.75s by rank, skillshot. Aim the carry (70%), clip the
  frontliner less often (45%). No setup spell required.
  Zyra ultimate: 1s knockup on both champions at 85% if they are grouped.
  Mikael's Blessing does not cleanse a knockup.
  Zyra plants keep a 6s zone on the fight and refresh Liandry after she steps back.

WHICH IS BETTER ON THIS BUILD
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Zyra. The damage lead is real early and small later. The CC lead is large
  at every minute. Fight healing ends in a tie, and it does not exist until Diadem.

  Damage at 8:00, Scythe + Ionian + Fated Ashes: Zyra 1752, Brand 1369 (28%).
  Damage at 20:00, Liandry + Helia: Zyra 2592, Brand 2466 (5%).
  Frontline at 20:00: Zyra 1109, Brand 935.
  Carry at 20:00: Brand 1531, Zyra 1484. Brand's detonation is the better single-target hit.
  At 23:00 with Harmonic: Zyra 2797, Brand 2689.

  Healing is 0 until Diadem at 22:00. That minute: Brand 1277, Zyra 1310.
  With Harmonic at 23:00: Brand 1699, Zyra 1740. An 8s fight fills Helia's per-second cap for both, so the fight heal ties.
  Lane heal/min at 23:00: Zyra 3542, Brand 1915.
  Plants keep Liandry burning between casts. Brand's W does not.
  That is the heal gap, and it is a lane gap.

  Hard CC at 20:00: Zyra 3.71s, Brand 1.13s.
  1.70s of Zyra's CC is a knockup, so Mikael's cannot cleanse it.
  Brand's 1.75s stun is the longer lock on one champion, and E is point-click,
  so the setup itself is reliable. It is still one cleansable stun, plus no zone.
  Bodies are the same: about 3000 HP, and effective HP is within 1%.

  On this item list Zyra does more of the things the items pay for:
  Liandry uptime, lane healing once Diadem exists, and crowd control.
  Brand is the better choice only when the draft wants that point-click stun.
