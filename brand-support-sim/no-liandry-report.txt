WHICH CHAMPION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Soraka is the best user of this list. At 18:00 the fight
  heal is 3266. The items multiply a heal that already exists.
  Brand, with no Liandry and no kit heal, restores 1418.
  Of that, Diadem is 193 and Helia is 1225.
  Removing Liandry takes away the burn Helia was converting, and Brand
  still has nothing of his own for Harmonic to copy.

  A tank can buy the items. The shop does not stop them. The items do
  not make them a tank.
  Leona's physical EHP on this build is 7191 with W up,
  against 9577 on a resist finish with the same W.
  Her fight heal is 645, all of it from Diadem and Helia
  (171 + 473). She has no ally heal to copy.
  Nautilus is the same shape: self shield, heal 759, EHP with W 6018.
  Braum's wall is not an ally shield, so his heal is 514.

  Three tanks actually spend the passives:
  Rakan Q and E: heal 799, shield 626, CC 3.0s, EHP 5079.
  Taric Q and W: heal 656, shield 344. His W shares bonus armor, and this build adds 0 armor, so Bastion
  does not make the carry tankier.
  Alistar's roar is a real ally heal (161 before items, 749 after) and his ultimate is percent damage reduction,
  so his EHP during R is 15308 without a resist item.
  That is the tank who can frontline on this list, because the tank
  steroid is in the ultimate, not in Locket.
  Galio deals 601 and heals 703. The W shield
  is his own, so Harmonic does not copy it.
  Thresh's lantern is a shield (511) and his
  souls keep armor the items do not give (EHP 4731).

  Amumu support can buy the same list. At 18:00 his fight heal is
  1080. Kit heal is 0. Diadem is 170 and Helia is 909.
  Damage is 1151 (Leona 558, Galio 601, Brand 1852).
  Hard CC is 3.6s (Leona 3.0s, Rakan 3.0s).
  Physical EHP is 4778, and Tantrum brings the fight to 4898.
  Leona with W is 7191. Alistar during R is 15308.
  Bandage and the ultimate are why he is picked. He has no ally heal and
  no ally shield, so Harmonic has nothing of his to copy and Helia opens
  only because Diadem ticks. Tantrum is a small flat reduction on himself.
  Soraka still heals 3266. Rakan still heals 799 and shields 626.
  Amumu is a niche engage pick on this build. He is not the champion the
  items are for, and the items do not make him a tank.

  The tankiest body on the Diadem list, steroid included, is Alistar at 15308 physical EHP.
  Suitability for the build is the heal column, not that EHP column.
  Soraka uses the items. A pure engage tank buys them and
  gives up the resists that make the role work. Rakan is the engage
  champion whose kit is already a heal and a shield. Alistar is the
  one who stays hard to kill during his ultimate.
