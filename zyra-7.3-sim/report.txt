shifted support boots to Ionian Lucidity (~80% pick) and mid
cores toward Blackfire → Liandry. This sim checks whether that
is actually better for plant-spam harass.

SUPPORT  (20-min plant-spam, Flash + Ignite)
  Items: Liandry → Ionian → Rylai → Morello
  Runes: Comet · Manaflow Band · Transcendence · Scorch · Cut Down
  Skill order: E → Q  (W 1-point, R at 5/9/13)
  Liandry online: ~9:00
  Rylai lock:     ~16:00
  Spells: Flash + Ignite (Heal if you need to pad the ADC).

  WHY
  • Liandry 2% max HP/s is still THE harass burn. Plants refresh it.
  • Ionian (1000g, 15 AH) is cheaper than Mana boots and feeds plant
    density. That is why live Diamond+ flipped off Boots of Mana.
    Do not upgrade to Crimson/Spellslinger until Rylai is done.
  • Rylai is still mandatory so the 3s burn and the 0.8s Comet land.
  • 4th item: Morello or Mandate actually finish on a 20-min gold
    curve. Blackfire 4th usually sits as Lost Chapter.
  • Mandate FIRST is the 7.3 'CC-mage support' bait — it delays
    the %HP burn. 3rd/4th only if the ADC follows the mark.
  • Trap: BF before Rylai. Paper burn, they walk out.
  • Trap: Rylai first. You lose the ~9:00 Liandry spike.

  RUNE NOTES
  • Comet over Aery/DH/First Strike. Roots + Rylai make the 0.8s delay
    land; Aery ticks more often but 5% AP on both is tiny after 7.2.
  • Manaflow + Transcendence + Scorch is the lane page.
  • Cut Down wins the poke metric (targets are >60% HP). Swap to
    Bone Plating vs kill lanes / all-in supports.
  • Gathering Storm is a 20-min trap on support — game is over.

MID  (22-min plant-spam, Flash + Ignite, Barrier vs assassins)
  Items: BF → Ionian → Liandry → Rylai
  Runes: Comet · Manaflow Band · Transcendence · Gathering Storm · Cut Down
  Skill order: Q → E  (W 1-point, R at 5/9/13)
  Blackfire online: ~7:00
  Liandry online:   ~13:00
  Rylai lock:       ~16:00

  WHY
  • Mid gold actually finishes Blackfire AND Liandry AND Rylai.
    Support could not. That is the whole role split.
  • Cheap Ionian (do not rush Spellslinger) so the Rylai lock and
    Void land on time. Spellslinger is the 22:00 peak if the lock
    is already done and you have gold left.
  • Crimson Lucidity is the dive/CD swap, not the damage boot.
  • Popular live core BF → Liandry → Zhonya is a survive-assassin
    swap. It loses harass to Rylai. Buy Zhonya 4th vs Zed/Fizz/Kat.
  • Oceanid → Banshee is a shield-shred/survive path, not the burn
    identity. Swap Oceanid in vs Lulu/Karma/Irelia shields.
  • Void after the Rylai lock is the tank spike. Deathcap after that.

  RUNE NOTES
  • Cut Down over Bone Plating into mages you poke off 90% HP.
    Bone Plating / Perseverance into assassins.
  • Botanist is gold/utility on river plants, not Thorn Spitters.
    Do not take it for 'Zyra plants'.
  • Axiom Arcanist is a 5% AoE ult bump. Scorch wins the lane.
  • Gathering Storm wins the 22-min average. Take Scorch unless
    you are playing for a long game.
  • Lane (1–10) winner: Aery / Manaflow·Trans·Scorch / Cut

  Shared traps
  • Comet is 5% AP after 7.2. Do not build 'for Comet'. Build for
    plants + Liandry; Comet is extra on the root.
  • Plants inherit YOUR magic pen (7.2). Spellslinger / Void scale
    the trees. Raw AP after the 10% plant ratio is weaker than pen.
