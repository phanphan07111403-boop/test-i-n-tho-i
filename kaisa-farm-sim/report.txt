================================================================================================
WILD RIFT KAI'SA — STATIKK FARM vs GUINSOO, AND STATIKK LAST
Patch 7.3 kit / 7.3b Statikk / 7.3f shop. Dragon lane, Q → E → W.
================================================================================================

QUESTION
  Guinsoo's Rageblade (the 'gusinoo' slot) is the single-target on-hit item.
  Statikk Shiv is the chain. How much farm does the chain actually buy,
  and what happens if it is moved to the last item the way Kalista can
  farm without it?

HOW THE CLEAR WORKS
  A Wild Rift wave is 4 minions, not 6. Statikk's bolt hits 4 targets from
  level 1 (3 extra bounces + the one you auto) for 90 magic on minions, and
  on-hits ride the bounces. Guinsoo hits the minion you clicked: +30 magic,
  and a phantom hit only after 4 Rageblade stacks, only on that minion.
  Q throws 6 missiles across whoever is standing there (12 if evolved) and
  does not reset on kill. Minions under 35% HP take 150% from those missiles.
  Kalista's Rend DOES reset on a minion kill, so she can front-load Guinsoo
  or Terminus and still chain the wave. Kai'Sa cannot copy that and keep
  the same shove.

VERDICT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  For the wave, finish Statikk before Guinsoo. Guinsoo first is the 1v1,
  and Statikk second still catches the shove at the same two-item gold.
  Do not move Statikk to the last slot.

  ONE ITEM, LEVEL 9 — THE CHAIN SHOVES, IT DOES NOT SKIP THE LAST MELEE
    Opening auto: Statikk 573 on 4 minions, Guinsoo 172 on 1.
    Casters die in 1.0s with Statikk vs 1.8s with Guinsoo. By one second the wave has lost 1367 HP vs 1118.
    Full standard clear is 6.6s vs 6.6s. That is a tie. Both are waiting on the 6s Q to finish the last melee.
    Cannon full clear 8.6s vs 9.0s. With 15 armor / 20 MR it is 9.6s vs 10.6s.
    Autos only, frontliner, 8s: Guinsoo 1346 vs Statikk 968. Guinsoo wins the duel. Statikk wins the first second.
    Neither item evolves Q (40 AD and 35 AD, gate is 70). Six missiles either way.

  SAME GOLD, TWO LEGENDARIES + GREAVES — THIS IS THE FORK
    Statikk + Guinsoo: Q evolved (40+35 AD), standard wave 3.4s, cannon 5.1s, 1687 HP gone at 1s.
    Guinsoo + Nashor: Q not evolved, standard wave 5.3s (35% slower), cannon 5.8s, 1373 HP at 1s.
    Nashor evolves W. A frontliner dies in 5.8s vs 7.2s on the chain, and auto-only damage is 2853 vs 2226.
    A squishy dies in 3.5s on the chain vs 3.8s on Nashor — 12 missiles win the short fight.
    So Nashor second wins the long frontline scrap. It does not win the
    squishy all-in, and it does not shove. The chain is the Q evolve.

  LAST SLOT
    On this no-kill clock Statikk is finished at 6.35 min if you rush it.
    Moved to last, it is still not bought at 26:00. A normal game is over.
    Q evolves at minute 16 on the rush (Guinsoo completes the 70 AD).
    With Statikk last, Q waits until minute 21 (Kraken's AD).
    W evolves earlier if Nashor is second: minute 15 vs 19.
    At level 15 the same five damage items, with the chain added, clear a
    standard wave in 1.6s vs 3.4s and a cannon in 2.4s vs 3.8s (37% faster on the cannon).
    The 90 magic bolt is not what does that. Guinsoo, Nashor and Terminus
    finally have a bounce. That spike only exists if the game lasts long
    enough to buy a sixth item.

  WHY KALISTA CAN AND KAI'SA CANNOT
    Kalista E refunds its cooldown on a minion kill, so a Guinsoo / Terminus
    order still chains the wave. One public baron list never buys Statikk
    (BotRK → Runaan → Guinsoo → Terminus) and still farms. Her dragon-lane
    list in 7.3d actually opens Statikk anyway, for the fight chain, not
    because she is stuck in the wave.
    Kai'Sa Q splits missiles and does not reset. Holding Statikk until last
    keeps her on 6 missiles until a later AD item, and the bounce that would
    spread Guinsoo / Nashor / Terminus never shows up in a 20-minute game.

  BUY
    1) Long Sword → Pickaxe → Kircheis → Aether Wisp → Statikk
       The bolt does not replace the last melee. It kills the casters on
       the first auto + Q, so the wave is already pushing.
    2) Berserker's Greaves
    3) Guinsoo's Rageblade — Q evolves here, and the duel damage shows up
    4) Nashor's Tooth — W evolves, and the chain starts spreading the on-hit
    5) Kraken Slayer, then Terminus
    Guinsoo first wins the 1v1 and reaches the same two-item inventory at
    the same gold, with a worse shove until the bolt exists. Statikk last
    is a Kalista habit: she refunds Rend on a minion kill. Kai'Sa spends
    the whole mid game on 6 missiles and a single-target auto.

  ASSUMPTIONS
    • Minion armor/MR default 0. The verdict reruns the cannon at 15 armor / 20 MR.
    • Energized opener is full (about 7s of walking at 340 MS fills 100).
    • Attack Energized is 6 + Electroshock 7. Move stacks are 1 per 24 units.
    • Bounce on-hits: Guinsoo 30, Nashor 15+20% AP, Terminus 30, Plasma.
      Not Kraken, not a second auto's AD, not a Rageblade phantom.
    • Phantom hit starts once 4 Guinsoo stacks are already up, every 3rd
      attack, and repeats on-hits including Guinsoo's own 30.
    • Q evolve counts bonus AD from items only. E evolve counts item AS only.
    • Aether Wisp 25 AP and Fiendish Codex 25 AP / 10 AH are component
      estimates. Finished-item stats are the patch numbers. The chain
      exists only on finished Statikk, not on Kircheis.
    • Lethal Tempo is off, so the fight gap is items and evolves, not the rune.
    • Gold has no kills. Both paths share it.
