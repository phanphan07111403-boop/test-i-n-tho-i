  WHY THIS PLAYSTYLE ITEMIZES THIS WAY
  • 7.3b: every Fury point is 0.5% crit from level 1. Full bar
    is 50% crit before items. R fills the bar. Overflow crit
    becomes 1 AD per 1% — IE's old Limit Break is gone, this is
    the champion passive, so a third crit item still pays.
  • Base crits are 200%. Infinity Edge makes them 230%. Trynd
    was buffed because he was not getting enough from that.
  • Spinning Slash crit-refunds on monsters (7.3) and on the
    champion (2s). Navori then cuts 15% of the remaining CD per
    auto, so the all-in is several spins, not one.
  • Q max, not E max. The refund already dumps E's cooldown.
    Bloodlust at rank 4 is +0.9 AD per 1% missing HP (+81 AD at
    the R threshold) and that AD crits and scales the spin.
  • Hullbreaker Skipper is 240% base AD + 9% max HP on turrets
    every 4th auto, and 7.3 turrets take 20% more from melee.
    BotRK does not hit structures. The duel item and the tower
    item are different slots.
  • Yun Tal is the farm item: 0 AD-crit until you auto, 0.4%
    per hit, cap 25%. A jungler who only farms caps it. Flurry
    is +35% AS once the duel starts. It does nothing to turrets.
  • Demolish over Nimbus. Ghost still gets you to the wave;
    Demolish is the plate. 85 + 28% max HP after 3 autos.

  MACRO
  • Full clear to level 5. E refunds on camp crits — that is
    the 7.3 farm buff. First back finishes item 1. Do not gank
    for a component.
  • Then catch the side wave on the half of the map your
    team is not fighting. Crash it, R whoever matches,
    auto the turret, leave when the second champion shows.
  • Press R before the CC, not at 1 HP. Q when R ends, not
    during it — spending Fury drops the crit you are hitting with.
  • You do not win the 1v2. Two answers = spin over the wall.
