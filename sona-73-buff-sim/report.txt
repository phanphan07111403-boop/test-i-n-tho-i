WHY 7.3 BUFFS SONA EVEN WITHOUT A CHAMPION HOTFIX
  • Crit damage 175% → 200% (IE 230%). Ardent's 30% AS buys extra crits.
  • AS cap 2.5 → 3.0, so the AS buff is not wasted on a stacked ADC.
  • Ardent 2700 → 2400, now a flat 30% AS + 25 on-hit (no level/crit gate).
  • New Echoes of Helia: Sona Q hits two champs → fills Soul Fragments.
  • New Circlet → Diadem: HSP + 0.8% mana/s pulse, Tear is 400g.

METRIC
  Buff impact  = extra ADC auto DPS from Ardent / Q aura / Staff  × combat%
  Sustain      = W heal+shield + Helia dump (×2) + Diadem pulse + Redemption
  Fight        = Mandate 7% mark + Power Chord/R lock-on-ADC
  Gold eff     = total impact per 1000g spent
  Combined     = 50% time-weighted impact + 30% gold-eff + 20% buff gold-eff
  Search       = every 3-item order of the 7 enchanter legendaries (210 paths)

  RECOMMENDED PURCHASE ORDER (20-min Sona support, crit ADC):
  1) Spectral Sickle → Black Mist Scythe (quest ~5:00)
  2) Boots of Speed (first back)
  3) Ardent Censer (~8:00) — 30% AS + 25 on-hit. Highest buff-GE vs a 7.3 crit ADC.
  4) Ionian Boots (~9:00) — aura lock / more W
  5) Echoes of Helia (~14:00) — Q×2 fills fragments, W dumps them into the ADC
  6) Imperial Mandate (~19:00) — 7% mark on Power Chord/R; 7.3 crit ADC multiplies it

  3rd-item note: #2 Ardent → Helia → Harmonic is within 0.02 GE. Mandate = more 7.3 impact; Harmonic = more heal buff.

  Skill order: W max (heal/shield/Ardent uptime) → Q max → E. R whenever.
  Early: 1 point Q at 1 for last-hit / Helia farm, then W.
  Runes: Aery · Font of Life · Bone Plating · Revitalize · Transcendence
  Spells: Flash + Heal  (Exhaust vs dive).
  Play: Q→W→E so auras never drop. W even at full HP (Ardent/Staff still proc).
  Power Chord the ADC's target — Font of Life + Mandate mark + 0.5s stun.

  Swap: ADC is AP (Kai'Sa/Ez) → Staff instead of Ardent.
        vs heavy CC → Mikael's instead of 3rd legendary (not in search).
        5v5 slugfest after 16 → Redemption 4th, not Mandate.

  Don't: Mandate/Redemption/Harmonic first — they spend 2400–2600g on
  fight-once effects and delay the 30% AS buff the 7.3 ADC actually uses.
