WHEN TO CHOOSE WHICH
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  IE is ability scaling. Q is multiplied by crit chance (up to 1.75x) and IE
  multiplies that bonus again (1.975x at 100% crit). Haste does not raise the
  multiplier. It raises how often Q is cast, how fast 225 stacks arrive, and
  how long Deathfire stays up once Q is an AoE. Shojin then adds up to +12%.

  Choose CRIT (ER → Berserker's → IE → RFC → LDR) when you can auto and you
  need range more than you need a health bar.
    IE completes at 17:00. Squishy kite that minute is 2717 vs ability 2102 (+29%).
    At 16:00 IE is not finished (Essence Reaver › Berserker's Greaves + B. F. Sword, Cloak of Agility, Pickaxe). Squishy kite is 2280 vs ability 2092 (+9%). Poke is already ability's: 1113 vs 899.
    At 22:00, with RFC, squishy kite is 3926 vs ability 2969 (+32%) and vs Cleaver-into-IE 3354. Poke is 1190 vs ability 1343 and vs Shojin-into-IE 1580.
    RFC's damage proc is small. The energized Q goes out from 700 range instead of
    550. That range is not in the damage columns. It is how this path avoids the dive
    the Live column assumes you ate. Skip RFC only when nothing outranges you.
    At 28:00 crit kite-squishy is 6418, behind Cleaver-into-IE (6922) and ahead of pure ability (4849). Crit chance on the crit path is 100%. Another crit item would not raise Q's multiplier.

  Choose ABILITY (ER → Ionian → Cleaver → Shojin → Hunger → Serylda) when you
  will get hit, you are mostly throwing Q, or an AD team needs the shred.
    Health at 22:00 is 2805 vs crit 1955. If the dive lands, uptime is 100% vs 70%. Messy damage (kite × uptime) is 2969 vs 2759.
    E is back in 11.7s vs 18.3s. Q cooldown is 1.87s vs 2.92s, so the 8s poke throws 4 Qs vs 3.
    The 225 burn and execute arrive at 23:00 on ability, 25:00 on Shojin-into-IE, and 27:00 on crit.
    Full Carve is 27% more physical damage for allies on the 22:00 tank.
    Personal tank kite at 28:00 is still lower: ability 4134 vs crit 5141 (-20%). Equal-stack tank kite is 4033, so the extra stacks are a small slice of that gap. The multiplier and LDR are the rest. Serylda is not finished; the pen in the bag is a Last Whisper (18%), not 35%.
    Heal over the 28:00 squishy kite is 364 on ability vs 160 on crit. Hunger and Gluttonous are the vamp; crit has Doran's 2.5% until a later item.

  Choose HYBRID when the game asks for both jobs in order.
    Shojin second, then IE. Take this when dive is real and you will outscale it.
    You do not have IE at 16:00 (ability-haste second). At 18:00 squishy kite is 2131 vs crit 3138. At 22:00, IE is in: poke 1580 (best of the five), squishy kite 2975, HP 2405, dive uptime 86%, E in 14.7s.
    At 28:00 Shojin-into-IE kite-squishies for 5992 and kite-tanks for 4956, with 3 Qs inside the kite.
    Cleaver second, then IE and LDR. Take this when the front line is the fight
    and someone else on your team is physical damage.
    At 22:00 tank kite is 2179 (crit 2435, ability 1935) and ally amp is 27%, at 85% dive uptime.
    At 28:00 it leads the gold-gated table: squishy kite 6922, tank kite 5880, HP 2755, dive uptime 86%. It still has no RFC, so that lead assumes 550 range.

  Navori is crit's way of buying Qs without giving up crit chance.
    At 28:00 the sheet Q cooldown is still 2.92s. The 8s kite fits 3 Qs on Navori and 3 on RFC crit, so this window does not show an extra cast. 6781 vs 6418 is the attack speed. Poke, with no autos to refund cooldown, is 2162 vs 2161.
    It does not replace Shojin. Shojin works from fog. Navori works while you auto.
    It also does not give the health Cleaver and Shojin give.

  The full-build table is the same fight after the last buy, stacks pinned at 250.
    Bloodthirster crit kite-squishy is 7977 and tank kite 6429, at 71% dive uptime. Cleaver-into-IE-LDR-RFC is 7554 / 6351 at 83% uptime. The 28:00 Cleaver lead was timing: crit had not bought the 80 AD yet, and Cleaver had no RFC.
    Finished ability poke is 3941 vs finished crit poke 2648, with dive uptime 97% vs 71%. Finished Navori gets 4 Qs in the kite vs 3, and kite-squishy 7575 vs Bloodthirster's 7977.

  Rule of thumb from these spikes:
    • You can free-hit squishies and you need 700 range — crit, IE before health.
    • You will only Q — haste first. Add IE when you can. Do not buy RFC to poke.
    • Assassin or engage will touch you — Shojin or Cleaver before IE.
    • Tanks, and your team is AD — Cleaver, then IE and LDR. Do not stop on Shojin.
    • Tanks, and you are the only AD — LDR is your pen. Cleaver's amp has no partner.
    • At 100% crit, stop buying crit. The next cloak does not scale Q.
    • Do not buy The Collector. The execute is already 6.5% max health.

  TRINITY (Tear → Trinity → Ionian → Manamune → Serylda → Shojin) is the
  no-crit ability path. Q stays at 1.00x. Tear is the first back, or the
  360 mana clock starts late and Muramana misses the mid game.
    Trinity completes at 09:00, Ionian at 11:00, Manamune at 16:00. Muramana turns on at 16:00 because Tear is already at 360 bonus mana.
    At 11:00, with Trinity and Ionian, squishy kite is 1592 vs ability 1249 (+27%) and vs crit 1692 (-6%). HP is 1588 vs 1405 vs 1255. This is the window the item is for: before Infinity Edge exists.
    Muramana's minute, poke is 1170 vs ability 1113 (+5%) and vs crit 899 (+30%). Squishy kite is 2069 vs ability 2092 vs crit 2280.
    At 22:00 Serylda is in and Shojin is not. Poke 1567 vs ability 1343 (+17%). Kite-squishy 3095 vs ability 2969 vs crit 3926. Tank kite 2070 vs 1935 vs 2435. HP 2438 vs ability 2805 (Shojin is already on that path). Dive uptime 88% vs 100% vs 70%.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ASSUMPTIONS THAT MOVE THE ANSWER
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Q crit is a ratio, not a roll. 100% crit = 1.75x physical. IE makes it 1.975x.
  • Autos crit for 200%, or 230% with Infinity Edge. Expected value, not a lucky fight.
  • Stack magic on Q is 25–55% of stacks from crit, 64% with IE. It is not crit twice.
  • W magic is 55% of stacks and does not care about crit. E magic does, bolts do not.
  • 225-stack burn is true damage from bonus AD and stacks. Crit does not increase the %.
  • Execute threshold is a flat 6.5% max HP. The Collector's 5% does not add anything.
  • Shojin's +12% ramps across the fight and applies to abilities, including the burn.
    It does not apply to autos, spellblade, Deathfire Touch, or Muramana Shock.
  • Trinity spellblade is 200% base AD on a 1.5s cooldown, and it does not restore mana.
    Essence Reaver is 125% base AD plus up to 50 from crit, and it refunds mana.
  • Awe is 2% max mana as bonus AD. Tear gains 27 bonus mana a minute while held,
    cap 360. Muramana replaces the item's mana with 1000 and turns Shock on.
    Q is a spell and an on-hit in one instance, so Shock uses the ranged ability
    ratio only (3% max mana). Autos are 1.2%. W, E, and R each proc it once.
  • After 25 stacks, Q is treated as AoE for Deathfire Touch (2s burn). Haste that
    holds Q under 2s keeps the burn up; a 2.9s Q lets it fall off between casts.
  • Cleaver is 6% armor reduction per physical hit, 5 stacks. Q and each auto add one.
    E's bolts fill it immediately. Allies get that shred; it is the Team column.
  • Rapid Firecannon range is the energized attack only: +35% range, capped at +150.
    Q inherits bonus attack range, so the proc Q reaches 700. Damage column adds the
    40 magic proc, not the range. Range is why you live, and it is not in the DPS.
  • Navori reduces remaining basic cooldowns by 15% per auto. Q itself is not counted
    as that auto. If Q does proc it in the live client, Navori is better than shown.
  • Endless Hunger Famine uses the ranged rate, 5 + 10% bonus AD ability haste.
  • All-in spends about 2s on R and E. Into armor those spells replace Qs and autos
    that would have hurt more, so tank kite is often higher than tank all-in.
    E is still the escape. The damage sim does not give it credit for dodging.
  • Dive uptime is a growing physical+magic burst against your HP, armor, and MR.
    Shieldbow would add its shield; none of these paths buy it. The Live column is
    'you got hit'. RFC's extra range is how the crit path makes that burst miss.
  • No Gathering Storm, no Jack of All Trades, no adaptive shards.
