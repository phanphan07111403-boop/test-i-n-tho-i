VERDICT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Top 5 Statikk Shiv users (Wild Rift 7.3, on-hit bounce rework):
    1) Varus
    2) Kog'Maw
    3) Kalista
    4) Teemo
    5) Twitch

  WHY THIS ORDER:
  • Shiv's unique job is copying on-hit onto the people you did not auto.
  • %HP on-hits (Kog W, Varus Blight) scale with tank HP in a 4-man pit.
  • Stack-then-detonate kits (Varus Q, Kalista E, Twitch E) cash extras in one spell.
  • 40 AP is real on Kog / Varus / Teemo / Kayle / Kai'Sa, dead on Vayne / Kalista.
  • Vayne Silver Bolts do not apply to extra targets (same rule as Runaan's).
  • Ashe's Frost is a slow, not a damage on-hit — Shiv is a utility toy on her.
  • vs Kraken: Kraken wins pure single-target. Shiv wins the clump. Buy Shiv
    when fights are grouped (dragon, Baron, mid siege), not for 1v1 lane.

  RECOMMENDED BUYS when the champ is in the top 5:
  1) Kircheis Shard + Berserker's (or Boots of Mana on Teemo/Kayle)
  2) Statikk Shiv (~9–11:00) — bounce comes online
  3) Guinsoo's Rageblade — phantom hit multiplies the on-hits Shiv copies
  4) Terminus (AD on-hit) or Nashor's Tooth (AP on-hit)
  5) BotRK if the frontline is thick

  Trap: buying Shiv on crit ADCs (Jinx, Caitlyn, Miss Fortune, Jhin).
  They wanted Magnetic Blaster. In 7.3 they want Stormrazor / RFC / IE.
