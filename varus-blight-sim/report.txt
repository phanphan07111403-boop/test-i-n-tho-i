==============================================================================
VARUS — STATIKK SHIV + RUNAAN'S HURRICANE (Wild Rift 7.3f)
Level 15. Q, W, and E are maxed. Full 1.5s Piercing Arrow.
Front target is the tank, then the bruiser, then the carry.
==============================================================================

Poke fires a full arrow after one auto, then repeats when Q is back.
Commit holds the arrow until the champion you are hitting has 3 Blight.
Charged means the first auto is the Statikk lightning. A wave in front
sends the bolts and the bounces into minions, so champions are not spread to.

ASSUMPTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Statikk is the 7.3b item: 40 AD, 40 AP, 30% attack speed, 2900 gold.
  At level 15 the lightning hits up to 6 extra targets for 60 magic damage
  and applies on-hit effects to those targets, not a second on-hit on the
  champion you attacked. Hurricane bolts are 55% AD, can crit, and apply
  on-hit to two champions in front. Both can hit the same side champion,
  so one lightning auto can put two Blight stacks on each of them.
  Energized is 6 per attack plus 1 per 24 units moved. Statikk adds 7 per
  attack. A full bar is the walked-in case. One attack range of walking
  is only 24 energy, so the lightning is not ready yet.
  Blight is 4.5% max health per stack at W rank 4, plus 1.2% per 100 AP.
  A full arrow adds 50% to that and to the arrow. The arrow itself loses
  15% damage per champion after the first, down to 33%. Blight does not.
  W's active is on the first arrow only. It is a 25s cooldown: 18% missing
  health at a full charge, from health missing before that arrow. Each stack
  refunds 13% of that ability's hasted cooldown, up to 39% per champion.
  Base crits deal 200% damage. Runaan's 25% crit is averaged, not rolled.
  Guinsoo's extra on-hit starts once it is at four stacks, every third
  attack, and the bolts copy that extra on-hit. Duskblade adds 160
  physical damage to the first attack, then on a 10s cooldown.
  Manamune's Awe is 2% of Varus's level-15 mana plus the item's 500.
  No Lethal Tempo, no legend runes, no Living Vengeance. Armor and magic
  resist are the targets above. Damage stops at 0 health.
