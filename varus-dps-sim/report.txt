========================================================================
VERDICT
========================================================================
Until LDR (~22:00) on-hit BotRK is ahead vs both targets: %HP + Blight + Rageblade phantom.
Late vs squishy TTK: on-hit still faster (3.00s vs 3.35s). Crit's higher 8s DPS (881 vs 830) is mostly overkill after the kill.
Late vs tank TTK: crit + LDR wins (7.00s vs 7.80s) — 100% crit at 230% crit damage plus 35% armor pen.
For games that end 15–20 min, buy on-hit. For games that reach 5 items, Yun Tal → IE → RFC → LDR is the 7.3 scaler (≈4x DPS from 6:00 vs ≈3.3x on-hit).
Highest 24:00 ceiling is Yun Tal → Hexoptics → IE (943/728 DPS) because Hexoptics is 55 AD, but it delays Infinity Edge ~4 min, so the mid-game curve is worse.

Crit 8s-DPS lead vs squishy holds from: 22:00
Crit 8s-DPS lead vs tank holds from:    22:00
Crit TTK lead vs squishy holds from:    never (on-hit kills squishies faster)
Crit TTK lead vs tank holds from:       22:00

Play pattern
- On-hit: auto to 3 Blight → E detonate → auto to 3 → short Q. Terminus dark stacks for 30% pen.
- Crit: keep autos up, charge Q on 3 Blight. Yun Tal needs ~125 autos (≈10 min of farming) to finish 25% crit.
- Do not mix: Rageblade phantom is the on-hit engine; IE wants 100% crit. 7.3 removed Rageblade's crit tax but phantom still does not replace IE.
