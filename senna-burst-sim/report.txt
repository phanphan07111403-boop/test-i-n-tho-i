  WHY DUSK → SERYLDA IS THE 2ND-ITEM OVERKILL:
  • Senna has no AD growth. The combo is bonus AD × Q/W/R
    (0.6 / 0.7 / 1.2) plus Relic 20% AD on-hit. Those spells
    DO NOT CRIT in 7.3 — only autos do, at 90% of 200%.
  • Item 1 Duskblade: 55 AD + 18 lethality + Nightstalker
    60–160. Already a kill threat with boots + a Dirk.
  • Item 2 Serylda: 50 AD + 35% pen. %pen applies to the
    WHOLE combo (R/W/Q/autos), then Dusk+boots lethality
    shaves the rest. Collector's 25% crit only multiplies
    2 autos, so Dusk→Collector is 104%/113% vs Serylda 119%/126%.
  • Expected damage (no lucky crits) still overkills both
    roles — you do not need a high-roll auto.
  • Isolated 2nd-item Δ ~697 is the biggest spike in the table.

  EARLIEST 2-ITEM KILL: Hexoptics → Dusk
    2nd legendary ~13:00 (a minute sooner). Hexoptics does
    not share Dirk/Caulfield with Duskblade, so the 2nd item
    completes earlier. ADC 1858 (110%) / mid 1988 (116%), also on expected.
    Take this if the game is ending at 13. Take Serylda if you
    want the fatter overkill vs anyone who bought a cloth.

  WHY 7.3 'MAGNETIC BLASTER REPLACEMENTS' MISS THE SPIKE:
  • RFC → Collector 2nd ~13:00  ADC lucky 1416 (84%) — RFC has 0 AD.
  • Fiendhunter → Collector 2nd ~13:00  ADC 1454 (86%). R-barrage guaranteed crits at 80%
    of normal crit, which is WORSE than Senna's native 90%
    modifier unless the auto was already going to crit.
  • Yun Tal → Collector 2nd ~14:00  ADC 1497 (88%). AS ratio is now 0.4 (real AS),
    but a 2.5s combo still only fits ~2 autos. Stacking 25%
    crit is a 3rd-item story.
  • Collector → IE 2nd ~14:00  ADC 1702. IE is 3500g so the spike is late, and 230% crit does not
    touch Q/W/R. Capstone after you already one-shot.
  • ER → Collector 2nd ~14:00  ADC 1582. Spellblade is 135% BASE AD. Senna's base AD is 54 forever.
  • Dusk → Youmuu 2nd ~14:00  ADC 1855. Two lethality items double dip flat pen on already-low
    armor; Serylda's 35% is the new multiplier.

  1) Long Sword → Serrated Dirk
  2) Duskblade of Draktharr   (~6:00, Nightstalker = item-1 spike)
  3) Boots of Dynamism        (+8 lethality)
  4) Serylda's Grudge         (~14:00, 35% pen — 2ND ITEM PEAK)
     Alt if the game is a 13-min stomp: Hexoptics → Duskblade
  5) Collector / Hexoptics / IE   (execute, range, or crit cap)
  6) GA / Mortal / Edge of Night

  Skill: max Q → W. R whenever. Combo: R (fog) → W root →
  Q (mist extract + Relic) → auto (Nightstalker + crit).
  Runes: Fleet, Empowered Attack, Brutal, Cut Down.

  Trap: RFC / Fiendhunter / Yun Tal first — 7.3 split Magnetic

  Trap: Essence Reaver — spellblade scales with base AD.
  Trap: Collector 2nd — crit/execute on 2 autos, spells ignore it.
  Trap: IE 2nd — 3500g delay + autos-only crit amp.
  Trap: Youmuu 2nd — more flat pen on armor that is already gone.
