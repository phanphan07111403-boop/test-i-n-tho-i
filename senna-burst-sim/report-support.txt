  WHY THIS PEAKS AT 2 AND STILL SCALES:
  • Fasting Mist is free crit (10%/20 stacks) + 1.25 AD/soul.
    Support hits ~100 Mist at 20:00 = 50% crit and 125 AD
    before items. Item crit stacks on top of that.
  • Q/W/R still do not crit. Mortal's 30% pen (or LDR 35%)
    is the 2nd-item overkill among crit items — same lesson
    as ADC Dusk → Serylda, on a crit chassis.
  • Collector 1st (3000): 50 AD + 10 lethality + 5% execute
    + 25% crit. Online ~8:00, already a kill threat with souls.
  • Skip Boots of Dynamism. 1200g delays 2nd item from ~14
    to ~16 and leaves you 400g short of IE at 20:00.
    Dynamism path 2nd ~16:00 (119% ADC), 20:00 no IE.
  • Late: IE 230% × Senna's 90% modifier = 207% autos, and
    soul crit is high enough that expected ≈ lucky.
  • Collector→Mortal 2nd ~14:00 (112% ADC); Hex→Mortal 2nd ~14:00 (110% ADC).
  • Hex→Collector 2nd ~14:00 (105% ADC) — no %pen, smaller spike.
  • Hex→IE 2nd ~15:00 (107% ADC); Collector→IE 2nd ~15:00 (107% ADC) — IE 2nd is a minute late.

  LETHALITY ON SUPPORT GOLD:
  • Dusk→Serylda 2nd ~16:00 ADC 142% / mid 150%.
  • At 20:00 lethality mix 150% vs winner 124%. Nightstalker is a flat proc; soul crit
    does not multiply it. Crit items ride the Mist curve.
    Pick Dusk→Serylda if you want raw burst and will leave
    the crit fantasy. This ranking is crit-only.

  TRAPS (same as ADC, worse on support gold):
  • RFC → IE 2nd ~14:00  ADC 98% — RFC has 0 AD.
  • Yun Tal → IE 2nd ~15:00  ADC 101% — stacking 25% crit is slow; souls already give crit.
    Yun Tal / Fiendhunter can look best at 20:00 once IE is
    up, but they miss the 2nd-item peak.
  • Essence Reaver: 135% of base AD 54. Skip.
  • Fiendhunter: 0 AD + 80% crit modifier after R. Skip.
  • Dynamism before item 2: gold sink on a crit build.
  • Galeforce 2nd is IE: looks strong 16–20 because 230% is
    already on, but the 14:00 Mortal spike is fatter.

  RECOMMENDED (crit support, 2nd-item peak, stronger late):
  1) Spectral Sickle → Black Mist Scythe (~5:00)
  2) The Collector           (execute + 25% crit)
  3) Mortal Reminder         (30% pen — 2ND ITEM PEAK)
  4) Infinity Edge           (late 230% — souls already crit)
     Alt if you want range first: Hexoptics → Mortal → IE
  Do not buy Dynamism before the 2nd legendary.

  Skill: max Q → W. Fasting: ADC last-hits, you take souls.
  Combo: R → W → Q → auto. Runes: Fleet, Empowered Attack,
  Brutal, Cut Down (Coup once they are chunked).
