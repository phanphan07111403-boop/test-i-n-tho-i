VERDICT

  Best WR on-hit path (bruiser-weighted): Rage → BotRK → Terminus (WR on-hit)
  Weighted avg: 2431 | 20:00 bruiser: 4672

  Worth it?: NO as the default. Playable as a mana-lane variant, not an upgrade.

  WHAT THE NUMBERS ACTUALLY SAY:
  • WR 7.3 Tear stacks fast (14 mana, 3×/10s). If you hold Tear from
    the first back, Manamune at ~6:00 is already Muramana. Stacking
    is NOT the PC problem. The problem is the item next to it.
  • Muramana first DOES win the 6:00 all-in vs Nashor first
    (1462 vs 1262). Shock + Awe AD hits
    hard before Aflame. That is the China spike, and it is real.
  • Rageblade first (~7:00) then beats both. 30 magic on-hit +
    Seething AS + later phantom hits is the WR 7.3 on-hit keystone.
    8:00: Rage 1949 vs Muramana 1712 (+237).
  • After that the China path is always chasing. Gold in Manamune
    is gold not in BotRK / Terminus. At 20:00 WR on-hit has
    Rage+BotRK+Terminus (4672); China has Rage+Nashor
    (4073) or Rage+BotRK still missing Terminus (4153).
  • Nashor → Dusk → Cap loses this auto-window metric. Dusk's extra
    on-hit is 2 procs / 6s; Rageblade phantoms scale with APS.
    Cap barely finishes in a 20-min WR game. Play Nashor-AP if you
    want Q/R burst and Deathcap, not if you want on-hit DPS.
  • Delaying Manamune (Nashor first, Tear second) is worse than
    rushing it. You pay the 2900g after the window where Muramana
    was actually ahead, and you still delay Rageblade/BotRK.

  WHY PC CHINA MANAMUNE IS A WORSE COPY THAN IT LOOKS:
  • WR Starfire on-hit is 5% bonus AD + 15% AP (PC E is much more
    AD). Manamune's AD barely feeds the passive; BotRK's %HP and
    Rageblade's flat magic do not care about that ratio.
  • Manamune has 0% attack speed. From level 9, Aflame waves want
    more attacks. Rageblade and BotRK both buy AS; Manamune does not.
  • Muramana Shock is 1.5% max mana on autos (~27–35 phys) and 3%
    on Q/E. Rageblade copies it on phantoms. Real damage — just
    less than 7% current HP (BotRK) into a 3k HP baron bruiser.
  • WR games are ~20 minutes. The China 3-item dream
    (Muramana+Rage+Nashor/BotRK) lands as the Nexus dies. The WR
    Rage+BotRK path spends the same gold on two AS on-hit items.

  WHEN TO STILL BUY IT:
  • Poke / mana-bully lanes where you want Tear Q-spam and will
    finish Manamune anyway. Then go Berserkers → Rageblade → BotRK,
    not Nashor. Accept ~5–12% less 6s DPS than Rage-first.
  • Do not buy Manamune as item 2 after Nashor. That path is a
    worse version of both identities.

  RECOMMENDED (WR baron Kayle, on-hit question, 20-min games):
  Default on-hit: Recurve + Pickaxe → Rageblade (~7:00)
                  → Berserker's Greaves → BotRK → Terminus
  Default AP:     Nashor → Mana boots → Dusk / Orb → Deathcap
                  (different fight: spells + Cap, not this 6s auto window)
  China import:   Tear → Manamune (instant Muramana ~6:00)
                  → Berserkers → Rageblade → BotRK
                  Only if you already wanted Tear. Not a DPS upgrade.

  Skill: max E → Q. Combat: Q shred → E reset → hold range.
  Level 5 ranged, 9 Aflame waves, 13 permanent Zeal.

  Trap: copying PC China Muramana+Rage+Nashor as the plan.
  WR 7.3 already gave on-hit Kayle Rageblade phantoms and BotRK
  %HP. Manamune is extra Shock, not the engine.
  Trap: Nashor → Manamune. You buy the China item after the only
  minutes where it was ahead, and you skip Rageblade.
