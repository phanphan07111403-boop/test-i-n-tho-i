7.3 CHANGES VS THE OLD 'NO YORDLE' READ
  • Yordle Trap Catcher now procs on slow OR immobilize.
    Winter's Bite (70% slow) lights it. R is no longer required.
  • Yordle / Locket / Zeke share Kindlegem + Cloth + Null.
  • Radiant Virtue is Cloth + Null + Giant's Belt (split from Vow).
  • Frozen Heart is a 25% AS aura, not Chill stacks.
  • Aftershock is gone. Ice Overlord is the immobilize keystone.
  • Guardian is the bodyguard keystone (shield you + the carry).
