  WHY AGGRESSIVE DH SENNA PEAKS THIS WAY:
  • Dark Harvest only procs below 50% — you must FORCE fights, not poke forever
  • Takedown resets CD to 1s — each kill snowballs souls HARD
  • Draktharr Nightstalker = dive burst + reset on takedown (soul printer)
  • Collector execute = finishes that create more DH resets
  • Magnetic / range tools keep you alive to chain the next <50% proc
  • Mist AD (1.25/stack) feeds both autos and the 10% bonus AD on DH
  • This is NOT support poke Senna — aggression > safe Q poke for souls

  RECOMMENDED PURCHASE ORDER (aggressive DH Senna, 20-min):
  1) Duskblade of Draktharr — FIRST spike, start reset chaining
  2) Boots of Dynamism
  3) The Collector — execute floor, more takedown resets
  4) Magnetic Blaster — range to keep farming DH after you get focused
  5) Serylda's Grudge
  6) Infinity Edge

  PLAY PATTERN TO MAX DH SOULS:
  • Look for 2v2 / river fights early — every takedown = free soul
  • AA → Q to apply Living Extraction, then finish with DH window
  • After first kill, immediately look for next <50% target (1s CD)
  • Wraiths from kills = Mist AD = stronger DH (10% bonus AD)
  • Trap: pure crit IE path — higher paper late DPS, fewer early resets
  • Trap: Manamune-first — safer poke, slower reset engine vs Drak rush
  • Trap: playing like enchanter Senna — you will starve Dark Harvest
