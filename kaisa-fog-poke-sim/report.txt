VERDICT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Best fog-poke path (avg 8–20 min 12s siege): Luden → Horizon → Cap
  Avg 12s vs squishy 8–20: 667 | 16:00 chunk 608
  W evolve (70% CD refund): ~7:00  ← the real spike
  Horizon Focus (+10% from 600+ range): ~14:00
  Luden's Echo (140+15% AP / 9s): ~7:00
  Deathcap (30% AP amp): ~20:00

  WHY FOG W WANTS THIS:
  • W is 30–120 + 110% AD + 60% AP. AP items are the ratio that scales poke.
  • Living Weapon on a finished AP item refunds 70% CD on champion hit.
    Unevolved W ≈ 14–20s. Evolved ≈ 4–6s. First legendary is the spike.
  • Luden 2800: evolve W at ~7:00 AND a 140+15% AP echo on the same missile.
    Horizon 2700 evolves in the same window but has no echo —
    12s siege at 8:00 is 425 (Luden) vs 310 (Horizon).
  • Horizon 2nd is the 3000-range tax: +10% Hypershot from 600+ units.
  • Liandry/Blackfire lose ~1s of the 3s burn after fog reveal.
  • Infinity Orb execute does nothing on a full-HP fog poke.
  • Malignance Hatefog is an ult zone — Kaisa R is a dash, not artillery.
  • Nashor evolves E (AS), not W — fog poke never autos.
  • Spellslinger T3 is almost a tie on avg, but delays Deathcap.

  RECOMMENDED BUY ORDER (fog W Kai'Sa, 20-min WR):
  1) Amplifying Tome + Boots of Speed
  2) Lost Chapter → Hextech Alternator → Luden's Echo  (~7:00)  W EVOLVE + echo
  3) Boots of Mana (25 AP, 8 flat mpen)
  4) Horizon Focus                                  (~14:00)  +10% long-range W
  5) Rabadon's Deathcap                             (~20:00)  30% AP amp
  6) Long game: Void Staff vs MR / Cryptbloom if you want AH+pen

  Situational swaps:
  • Want +10% ASAP: Horizon first, Luden second (weaker 7–13, equal later).
  • They stack MR: Void 3rd instead of Cap.
  • They are <35% HP in fog: Orb 3rd for Inevitable Demise.
  • Do not first-buy Nashor / Malignance / Rylai / Liandry for fog W.

  RUNES (strongest poke — slot 3 is Storm OR Scorch, not both):
  Keystone: Arcane Comet     — extra chunk on a 3000-range missile
  Slot 2:   Transcendence    — AH → more evolved W
  Slot 3:   Gathering Storm  — AP every 3 min after 6:00 (20-min games)
             Scorch only if the game is a 12-min stomp
  Alt key:  First Strike     — 7% true + gold, slightly less raw W
  Avoid:    Electrocute (3 hits), Conqueror, Lethal Tempo, Aery
  Spells:   Flash + Ghost (rotate fog) or Flash + Barrier
  Skill:    Max W → E → Q. R when available. Start W.
