VERDICT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ADC / flex DH: Boots of Dynamism → Armorcrusher (+10.7% combat area).
  Support: damage +10.5% Dynamism, heal +10.7% Ionian, Q/min +14.0% Ionian.

  WHY THE ROLE SPLIT HOLDS:
  1) Dynamism is AD + pen. Senna's Q, autos, Relic Cannon, Living Extraction,
     and Dark Harvest (10% bonus AD) are all physical. Pen multiplies the kit.
  2) Ionian is 15 AH. Transcendence already gives 12. Draktharr adds 10.
     Helia + Harmonic add 40 more on support. Extra Q/min is real, smaller
     than 15 AD + 10 pen on a champion whose damage is auto+Q.
  3) Dynamism eats the Long Sword ADC already bought — T2 at 2:00,
     Draktharr 8:00. Ionia does not use that sword, so 500g sits leftover
     and Draktharr slips to 9:00, Collector to 14:00. Support has to BUY
     a Long Sword just for Dynamism; Ardent still ~9:00, T3 16:00 vs 15:00.
  4) Ionian Flash (T2 15% / Crimson 20%) is the support reason, not DPS.
     150s → ~130s → 125s. W roots and Q heals also scale with haste.
  5) Armorcrusher's 6% pen is the T3 identity. Crimson's T3 identity is
     Flash + MS after Q heal — peel, not execute.

  BUY DYNAMISM WHEN:
  • Bot / flex carry, Dark Harvest, you auto then Q (AA→Q).
  • First legendary is Draktharr / Collector / Youmuu — pen stacks.
  • You already have a Long Sword in lane.

  BUY IONIAN WHEN:
  • Support. Q is a heal. Flash + W root are the job.
  • You already hit haste from Helia / Harmonic / Mandate and still want
    Flash 125s more than 20 AD.
  • You cannot auto (max-range poke/heal only) — then AH is the damage stat.

  TRAPS:
  • 'Senna needs haste' → Ionia. She needs Q uptime. Autos already refund
    1s. Transcendence + Draktharr/Helia cover most of the 15 AH.
  • Copying ADC Dynamism onto support. Heal/Q/W/Flash lose; damage is selfish.
  • Copying support Ionia onto DH carry. Leftover Long Sword delays Draktharr;
    0 pen until Collector/Serylda.
  • Buying T3 before 10:00 — the shop will not allow it.
