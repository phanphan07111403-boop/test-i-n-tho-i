  WHY 7.3 LATE SMOLDER IS A CRIT PAGE:
  • Q damage is increased by crit chance, then again by bonus
    crit damage. Wiki: 0–45% (+0–13.5% from IE) based on crit.
    At 100% crit + IE that is a 58.5% multiplier on Super Scorcher
    physical, and Dragon Practice magic goes 30% → 69% of stacks.
  • Patch 7.3 raised base crit damage 175% → 200% and made IE
    the capstone (75 AD, 230% crit damage). Magnetic Blaster is gone.
  • Q applies on-hit, so Essence Reaver Spellblade rides on the
    fireball. That is the first-item Q identity.
  • Hexoptics C44 is the Magnetic Blaster replacement that actually
    scales the poke: 10% damage at 550 range — Q's exact range.
  • Old Mura→Tri→Serylda at 22:00 mix 4999; ER → IE → Hex → LDR → BT 6066 (+21%).
    25:00 mix 5713 vs 7301 (+28%). Poke-squish 5269 vs 6776.
    Muramana still shocks, but 0% crit means Q never gets the
    7.3 amp. IE arrives as item 5 and the page is already behind.

  SHOJIN vs 4TH CRIT ITEM:
    Shojin 12% ability amp @ 75% crit → mix 6442.
    Hexoptics 4th (100% crit + 10% range amp) → mix 6489.
    25% extra crit on Q is ~+11% Scorcher amp plus auto crits;
    Shojin's 12% only touches abilities and delays 100% crit.

  NAVORI vs HEXOPTICS:
    Navori Qs @22: 5 | Hexoptics Qs @22: 4
    25:00 poke-squish Navori 6164 vs Hex 6358.
    Navori has 0 AD. Extra Qs do not beat Hexoptics' 55 AD +
    10% max-range amp on every Scorcher.

  RECOMMENDED (strongest late, patch 7.3):
  1) Long Sword → Sheen
  2) Essence Reaver   (~9:00)  — Spellblade on Q, 25% crit, 20 AH
  3) Ionian Boots of Lucidity  — more Qs (Greaves if you need vamp)
  4) Infinity Edge    (~14:00) — THE late item (230% crit damage)
  5) Hexoptics C44    (~18:00) — 10% damage at Q range + 25% crit
  6) Mortal Reminder / LDR     — 100% crit + %pen
     Mortal if they heal; LDR if they stacked HP/armor
  7) Bloodthirster             — 75 AD (no crit; you are already 100%)
     Shieldbow instead vs dive. Maw vs heavy AP. GA vs assassins.

  Skill: max Q → W → E. R at 6/11/15.
  Stacks: last-hit with Q, poke champs for extra stacks. T3 ~17:00.
  Poke: Q from 550. Hexoptics is already at max amp. Don't E in.
  Fight: Q weave, W explosion, R through the clump (and yourself).

  Trap: Muramana → Trinity → Serylda. That was 7.2. In 7.3 Q wants
  crit chance and IE; the mana page has neither until item 5.
  Trap: Shojin 2nd. Ability amp is real, but it delays 100% crit
  and the Hexoptics range amp that is Smolder's poke identity.
  Trap: Collector. Smolder already executes at 6.5% with T3 burn.

  RUNES THAT FIT THIS PAGE:
  Fleet / Zeal / Cut Down / Bloodline / Transcendence
    22:00 mix 6066 | 25:00 7301 | Qs @22 4
  • Fleet Footwork — Q is on-attack, so the fireball procs the
    heal / 20% MS / missing-mana. That is how you live to 175 stacks.
    This page buys 0 attack speed; Fleet's job is uptime, not DPS.
  • Battle Zeal — +2%/s basic-ability damage, cap 6%. Q/W/E and
    the T3 burn are basic-ability damage. Brutal only spices the
    on-hit; Zeal 25:00 mix 7139 → 7301.
  • Cut Down — poke hits healthy targets. Coup is the same slot
    and overlaps the 6.5% T3 execute (poke-squish 6362 vs 6776).
  • Legend: Bloodline — 7% omnivamp heals off Q magic + true burn.
    BT lifesteal does not. In an 8s window Haste is the same 4 Qs
    (7301 mix) — take Haste only
    if you already have enough vamp and want W/E/R uptime.
  • Transcendence — 10 AH + 8% refund. Dropping it for Bone Plating
    costs a Q in the window (3 vs 4). Bone Plating only vs Lucian/Draven/Leona
    until you can swap back.
  • Conqueror paper-leads 25:00 mix (7746) by stacking AD in combat.
    Hexoptics poke is max-range; you are not in Conqueror range.
  • Lethal Tempo 7.3 wants AS items. ER/IE/Hex/LDR/BT is 0% item AS.
    LT 25:00 mix 7640 is fight autos,
    and it does not stack during a Q siege.
  • Phase Rush is the dive/gank swap (common on CN screenshots).
    0 damage. Take it when you cannot stand still to stack.
