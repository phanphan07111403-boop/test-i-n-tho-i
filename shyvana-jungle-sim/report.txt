------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Best path (counter meta + win late): TF → BotRK → DD → FoN → Twin
  Late-weighted avg: 6741 | 25:00 late-power: 10269
  Spellblade core online: ~8:00
  Anti-tank shred online:  ~16:00  (BotRK or Liandry)
  Brawl tank item online:  ~21:00

  WHY LATE-GAME META NEEDS SHRED + SURVIVE:
  • Patch 7.3d cut Ocean dragon omnivamp 20% → 10%. Pure damage
    builds no longer outheal dive pressure in a full 12s form.
  • Meta frontlines stack HP/armor. Without BotRK (Q double on-hit)
    or Liandry (2% max HP/s), dragon form tickles tanks.
  • Flame Breath ratios were buffed (50% AD + 80% AP; dragon E
    100%/100%). AP burn paths gained real late DPS — but still
    need a tank item or they get deleted by Lillia/Yi/assassins.
  • Scorch becomes TRUE damage at 300 Draconic stacks — dragons
    are still the win condition; itemization must keep you alive
    long enough to auto the marked target.

  vs runner-up (TF → BotRK → Twin → FoN → DD):
    25:00 late-power 10269 vs 9045 (+14%)
  vs anti-tank glass (TF → BotRK → Eclipse → Serylda → Wit):
    20:00 6335 vs 6990 (-9%) | 25:00 10269 vs 10042 (+2%)
    alive 25:00 12.0s vs 8.6s — glass shreds harder on paper (14185 tank-dmg) but dies mid-form
  vs AP burn (Dusk → Nashor → Liandry → Rift → Shojin):
    25:00 late-power 10269 vs 8359 (+23%) | alive 12.0s vs 9.0s

  RECOMMENDED (counter tanky meta, win late dragon fights):
  1) Long Sword → Sheen / Phage / Hearthbound
  2) Trinity Force           (~8–10:00) — clear + Spellblade
  3) Immortal Treads / Berserker's
  4) Blade of the Ruined King — Q applies %HP twice (anti-tank)
  5) Death's Dance OR Twinguard next — Ocean nerf → buy survive
  6) Force of Nature / Randuin — match their damage type
  7) Twinguard / Sterak — never skip a resist spike for 6th DPS

  Runes: Conqueror (long fights) or Fleet Footwork (stick + heal).
  Secondary: Unshakeable + Overgrowth / Second Wind + Alacrity.
  Skill: max E → W → Q; R at 5 / 9 / 13.

  Play: full clear → dragons for stacks → R AFTER they commit.
  Combo: R land → E mark → W stick → Q double on-hits → autos.
  Trap: Kraken / Cap glass — paper late DPS, dies at 6–8s of form.
  Trap: Shojin → Hex damage path without BotRK — tanks live forever.
  Trap: rushing Twinguard before shred — you live, tanks also live.
