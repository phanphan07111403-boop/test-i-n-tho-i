FLEET FOOTWORK vs LETHAL TEMPO — KITING ON A PAD
Wild Rift 7.3  |  GameSir X3 Pro  |  stick held away, RT tap, Follow OFF
Window: 8.0s  |  CAUGHT = melee reaches 175  |  KILL = that body dies  |  up = 8s, still apart
Body: 2800 HP, 80 armor, 45 MR. Damage is post-mitigation and can overkill.
==============================================================================

WHAT THE PAD IS DOING
  Analog kite = left stick away between autos, RT on each attack.
  Wind-up: you stand. A new move in that window cancels the shot.
  Cooldown after the shot: this is the walk. This is the kite.
  Correct map (50 ms, Mapping Enhancement on): the next auto is LATE.
    That lateness is extra walk. Hands below called 'pad' (tap floor 0.36s).
  Broken map (RT drops the stick): each auto adds a 0.05s stand. 'pad stuck'.
  'mouse' is the same champ with no delay and no tap floor.
  7.3 Tempo is +6.4% AS per stack, 6 stacks, then a bolt. No bonus range.
  Fleet is +20% move speed for 1s, a heal, and +40% AS on that one auto.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CADENCE — can the shoulder button spend Tempo?  (Kai'Sa, no E)
  period = seconds per auto at 0 Tempo stacks and at 6.
  walk   = period minus wind-up at 6 stacks. That is stick time.
  A 0.36s tap floor wastes AS only when the 6-stack period is shorter.

VERDICT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  On a pad, take Tempo unless Supercharge is what keeps the fight going.
  7.3 Tempo is slow enough to tap. It does not create space.
  Fleet's 20% move speed does, but only after the energize bar
  fills — a long fight, not a 2-second run-down. A ghost on
  Kai'Sa ends before either rune turns on.

  KILL = you killed the 2800 HP body. CAUGHT = they reached 175.
  up = the 8s window ended with you untouched. Heal 0 means Fleet

  PAD, NOT THE RUNE, IF THE MAP IS WRONG.
    'pad stuck' freezes you 0.05s on every RT. That is Mapping
    Enhancement off, or the joystick not Locked. Both runes get

    Wind-up 18% Kai'Sa / 22% Ashe, and it scales 1:1 with attack
    speed. Not in the 7.3 notes. The stress table (modifier 0.6)
    is the case where bonus AS makes you stand a larger share
    of each auto. There Tempo gets caught sooner vs a walker
    you could previously hold. Treat that as a risk, not the
    base answer.

    Both pages can take Ghost. It is not in the numbers.
    Kai'Sa E cast time (1.0s → 0.5s with bonus AS) is the wiki
    value. 7.3 reprinted the move-speed formula, not the cast.
    Berserker AD (10) is the approximate value from the item pass.
    Ashe slow is a flat 20% for 2s. The slow, not the rune, is
