  WHY THE KIT ITEMIZES THIS WAY
  • Vitals are true damage: 3.5% + 5.5% per 100 bonus AD of
    their max health. R marks all four for 8 seconds. Bonus AD
    is what moves the tank fight. Percent pen multiplies Q,
    autos, Spellblade, and BotRK.
  • Q max, then E. Lunge is 85–115 + 110–125% bonus AD, refunds
    half the cooldown on hit, applies on-hit, and hits turrets.
    Bladework's second hit always crits for 170–200% AD.
    W is a 0.75s parry and it is maxed last.
  • Eclipse (7.3f, 60 AD) procs 7% max HP physical and a shield
    of 150 + 40% bonus AD, twice in an ultimate. Trinity Spellblade
    is 200% base AD. Sunderer is 10% max HP and a 6% heal;
    structures take half of that proc. Its 25 AD feeds a smaller vital.
  • Hullbreaker on a turret is 240% base AD + 9% of your max HP
    every 4th auto, and the crash lasts one extra second. On a
    champion it is 160% base AD + 5% of your max HP. BotRK's
    on-hit ends at the champion; the turret is Hull, Trinity, Q, Demolish.
  • Morde steals 10% of your AD and resists during his R, and
    his damage is magic. The ult heal covers that when four
    vitals land. Teemo's blind cuts autos and on-hits and costs
    one vital. Three vitals and Q still kill him in this all-in.
  • A cannon crash is one-target Q plus autos. Haste and attack
    speed are the clear.
  • Demolish is 85 + 28% max HP after three autos. Every row wears
    Steelcaps: 25 armor, 150 HP, 10% less from champion autos.
    Gluttonous is the popular 1000g boot (12 AD, omnivamp).
    Steelcaps are here because Malphite, Shen, and Kalista auto the wave.

  MACRO
  • Teleport to the side wave your team is not fighting. Crash
    it, R the one champion who matches, auto the turret.
  • Two champions on the wave ends the crash. Riposte parries
    one spell — Malphite ult, Shen taunt, Ornn knockup, Morde
    pull — and then you leave.
  • The ult heal is 80/110/140 + 60% bonus AD per second, and
    it lasts longer as the vitals land. The sides of the
    ultimate are the fight.
  • Mundo heals harder than the tank average in this sim.
    Executioner's is the item for that specific champion.
