  WHAT EACH SECOND ITEM ACTUALLY CHANGES
  • Guinsoo is the single-target item. After four attacks, Seething Strike is
    +32% attack speed and every 3rd attack repeats W, BoRK, and the 30 magic
    on-hit. W reads max HP, so the repeat still hurts a tank that is already low.
    The 30 AP is a small W/Q bump, not the reason to buy it.
  • Runaan does not add a second hit on the champion you clicked. It adds two
    bolts. Each bolt is 55% AD, can crit, and applies W and BoRK at full value
    to whoever is standing beside the target. On a solo frontliner the bolts
    hit nothing. In a clump or a wave they are the item.
  • The physical auto is close either way. Runaan's 25% crit at 200% damage
    covers most of Guinsoo's extra 35 AD. The split is Phantom Hit vs splash.
  • Solo-tank healing is almost the same. In the 3-champion fight Runaan
    heals roughly two thirds more, because each bolt procs lifesteal and the
    Greaves on-hit. That is extra time alive, not extra damage on the tank.
  • A 3-auto trade never reaches Phantom Hit. Finished Guinsoo still wins that
    trade on AD plus the 30 magic on-hit, by a small margin. The large tank
    gap shows up across the full Barrage, not in one lane trade.
  • Dragon caps W at 100 and caps BoRK at 100. Phantom Hit still raises
    objective damage, just not as a percent-health melter. Waves do not care
    about that cap: Runaan hits three minions per auto, Guinsoo hits one.
  • Capped kill damage hides this. Once both builds kill the tank inside 8s,
    total HP removed matches. Time-to-kill and the first 4 seconds do not.
  • At 17:00 both items are owned and the paths deal the same damage. Runaan
    second spends 2650 and comes online one wave earlier. Guinsoo second spends
    3000 and is the tank/dragon item for the four minutes after that.

  BUY
  • Guinsoo's second when one champion is the win condition: the tank you
    have to cut through, or the dragon you have to burn. You kill that target
    faster inside the same Barrage. You do not wipe the people beside him,
    and the wave takes most of W plus a little more.
  • Runaan's second when they stand on top of each other or you need to shove
    and move. The clumped fight actually ends. The wave dies inside one W.
    The solo tank still dies during Barrage, about a second and a half later.
  • Either way the other item is next. Order stops mattering the moment both
    are finished. Crit on Runaan does not replace Phantom Hit on a lone tank.

  HOW TO READ THE PROC COLUMN
  Proc is Energized chains during the 5-champion fight. Each chain can touch
  up to 6 other units at level 13 and puts W, BoRK, and Guinsoo's 30 magic
  on-hit on them once. Runaan puts those on-hits on 2 units on every auto.
  Shiv costs 3000. Runaan costs 2650. Terminus and Wit's End are the other
  last-slot damage items if the game actually reaches a 5th legendary.
  Even-gold core (BoRK + Greaves + Guinsoo + Runaan) is 9650 and finishes
  at 17:00. Adding Shiv makes 12650. Gold at 18:00 is 10770.

  SHIV VERDICT
  • Skip it. On-hit Kog already has the spread item: Runaan hits 2 extra
    units on every auto. Shiv's chain fires about twice per Barrage, so the
    6-target bounce does not replace that.
  • Guinsoo + Shiv, with no Runaan: the 3-champion fight is still alive
    when Barrage ends. Guinsoo + Runaan wipes that fight in 5.8s. The wave
    goes from 3.4s to 6.5s. The solo tank is only ~0.3s faster.
  • Guinsoo + Runaan + Shiv does wipe a 5-champion clump inside Barrage
    (7.1s). Terminus in that same slot also wipes it (7.1s) and kills the
    tank in 3.6s instead of 4.0s and the dragon in 5.7s instead of 6.9s.
  • An even 18:00 game has 10770 gold. The 4-item core is 9650. The 3000g
    Shiv does not fit unless you are ahead or the game runs long, and the
    long-game slot is Terminus when a frontliner is the problem.

ASSUMPTIONS
  • 7.3: Guinsoo 3000g, 35 AD / 30 AP / 30% AS, 30 magic on-hit, +8% AS × 4,
    Phantom Hit, crit no longer disabled. Runaan 2650g, 40% AS / 25% crit /
    4% MS, two bolts at 55% AD. BoRK 3100g, 40 AD / 30% AS / 12% lifesteal,
    7% current HP. Greaves 35% AS and 10 heal on-hit, 900g. Crit damage 200%.
  • AS cap 3.0. Kog ratio 0.665, base 0.665, base bonus 0.2, +0.03/level
    on the 7.3 curve. Q passive AS is always on. W is 8s, pressed at the start.
  • Bolts apply on-hit at 100% and can crit. They do not spawn more bolts,
    do not stack Guinsoo or Lethal Tempo, and do not carry Brutal.
  • Phantom Hit repeats on-hit on the primary only, using HP after the first hit.
  • Runes: Lethal Tempo, Brutal, Cut Down (6.5% vs targets above 60% HP),
    Legend: Alacrity stacked by ~8:00. No Coup de Grace.
  • You land Q on the focused champion (4s shred, 7s cooldown). Side targets
    and minions are not shredded. No misses, no CC, expected crit, not a coin flip.
  • Lifesteal applies to the auto, the bolts, and on-hit damage. Flat Greaves
    heal procs on each on-hit application, including bolts and Phantom Hit.
  • Zeal component is 15% AS / 15% crit. Recurve still has 15 physical on-hit
    until the legendary consumes it. Wind Blade's old 15 flat damage is omitted;
    the 7.3 item card does not list it.
  • Statikk Shiv: 40 AD, 40 AP, 30% AS, 4% MS, 3000g. At level 13 the chain
    hits 6 extra targets for 60 magic (90 vs minions and monsters), no crit.
    Bounces apply on-hit once and do not repeat Phantom Hit.
  • Energized cap is 100. Base gain is 6 per attack and 1 per 24 units
    moved; Shiv adds 5 per attack. Kiting moves for 60% of the gap between
    autos. Patch 7.3 does not republish that base gain.
