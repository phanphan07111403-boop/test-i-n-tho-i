Runes (legal page — the listed Sorcery trio does not fit)
---------------------------------------------------------
Precision  Fleet Footwork → Presence of Mind → Legend: Alacrity → Coup de Grace
Sorcery    Absolute Focus + Gathering Storm
Shards     Adaptive, Adaptive, scaling health

Fleet, Presence of Mind, Alacrity, and Manaflow add no damage to this
combo. Fleet keeps you above 70% HP so Absolute Focus stays on.
Alacrity is how you get the 3 setup autos off. Manaflow cannot be taken
with both Absolute Focus and Gathering Storm (Sorcery secondary is 2
runes, one per row). Presence of Mind is the mana rune that does fit.
Finish rune is Coup de Grace — it won the all-in on this target.

Combo that the spikes are measured on
--------------------------------------
All-in: 3 autos apply Blight → R from at least 600 range detonates them
and applies a fresh set → hold Q through the root (W during the channel)
and release fully charged. Pick (no autos) is the same R → W+Q when you
cannot walk up. Blight is 2.5–4.5% max HP per stack (+1.4% per 100 AP),
×1.70 on a full Q. W active is 6–10% missing HP per stack, ×1.70, no AP
ratio. Numbers are post-mitigation and keep counting after the kill, so
a later item still shows a bigger combo once you already one-shot.

Nashor's Tooth first
-------------------
Best order that starts Nashor's, same search:
  Nashor's Tooth → Shadowflame → Void Staff → Stormsurge → Rabadon's Deathcap
  Boots slot: 0. Finish rune: coup.
  All-in at item 3: 184.4% HP vs the winner's 210.9%.
  All-in at item 5: 273.8% HP vs the winner's 306.9%.
Nashor is the attack-speed / on-hit item. This combo's damage is
the Blight pop, and R already applies the stacks, so a pen or AP
legendary finishes harder. Buy Nashor only when ult is down often
enough that the 3 autos are the whole trade.

Assumptions
-----------
- Patch 26.21 Varus: Q full-charge amp is 70% (was 50%). W on-hit is
  4–28 (+20% bonus AD) (+30% AP). Blight is 2.5/3/3.5/4/4.5% max HP
  per stack (+1.4% per 100 AP). W active is 6/7/8/9/10% missing HP
  per stack, also ×1.70 at full charge, and does not scale with AP.
- R applies Blight at 0.65s / 1.20s / 1.75s of the root. A squishy with
  no tenacity gets 3. Mercury's Treads (30% tenacity) cut that to 2.
  Cleanse or QSS before 0.65s applies zero stacks and the combo fizzles.
- Horizon Focus amps the R cast and everything after it. Setup autos
  happen before the mark. The R is taken from ≥600 range; from melee
  the 10% does not start.
- Shadowflame is +20% magic damage on hits that start below 40% HP.
  Cut Down is +8% while the target is above 60% HP. Coup de Grace is
  +8% while they are below 40% HP. They apply per hit, not to the combo
  as a lump.
- Stormsurge Squall (125 + 10% AP) counts when the R → W+Q stretch
  deals 25% of max HP. Luden is the isolated-target dump, 150 + 10% AP,
  once. Liandry is 2% max HP per second for 3 seconds.
- Lich Bane's spellblade needs an auto after the spell. Q's channel
  locks attacks, so the proc is not in this combo.
- Deathcap is +30% AP, including runes. Percent pen stacks multiplicatively.
  Flat pen can push MR below zero.
- Gold is an unfed farmer. Sorcs and the first legendary land around
  5:00 and 12:00, the fourth legendary in the high 20s, the fifth around
  33:00. A kill lead finishes the same order sooner.
- Same-item dips of a few hundred damage are the 40% execute line
  (Shadowflame and Coup de Grace) flicking off as the target gains HP.
- Skill order is Q start, then W max, Q, E. R at 6/11/16.
