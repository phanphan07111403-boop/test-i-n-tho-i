VERDICT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Buy: Armored → Divine → Navori → Riftmaker → Wit's End → Unending

  WHY THIS IS THE LATE-GAME VOLIBEAR ON WILD RIFT:
  • The wounded bite is 1.60× the normal maul. Bonus AD does not raise
    that multiplier. AD still feeds the 110% / 176% ratios, Q and R.
    Bonus HP feeds the 6.5% / 10.4% ratios and the E shield.
  • Spirit Visage is not in the game. Healing is the raw W heal,
    spellblade heals, and whatever Lifeline he bought.
  • Navori is how W comes back inside the ultimate. Each attack cuts
    15% off the remaining Q, W and E cooldown. The 25% crit is on the
    attack, and crits deal 200% damage on this patch.
  • Divine Sunderer is 10% of the target's max HP on the spellblade
    and a heal for 6% of their max HP, on a 1.5s cooldown.
  • Riftmaker turns 2% of bonus HP into AP. After 4 seconds in the
    fight it is +8% damage and 10% omnivamp.
  • Wit's End is 50% attack speed, 45 magic resistance and a 40
    magic on-hit. Patch 7.3 removed its heal.
  • Unending pulses 3% max HP magic every 4s and heals for 250% of
    the post-mitigation damage, once per enemy standing in it.
  • R's bonus HP is bonus health for the bite and the E shield. The
    no-ult column keeps a 12-second stat stick from winning the search.

  Vs Full Tank (Armored → Heartsteel → Unending → Randuin → Kaenic → Twinguard):
    5090 in 11.0s, died=False, end 84%.
  Vs AP Glass (Mercs → Dusk → Nashor → Deathcap → Infinity Orb → Void):
    9150 in 6.7s, died=True, end 0%.

  ANTIHEAL:
    With 60% grievous the winner still stands (11.0s, end 50%, 9150 damage).

  RUNES:
    Conqueror, Last Stand, Alacrity, Overgrowth, Brutal
    Conqueror is 5 AD or 8 AP per stack at level 15, six stacks,
    then 9% omnivamp. The fight starts at zero stacks.
    Last Stand is 5–11% damage below 60% HP. Alacrity is 21% attack
    speed once it is stacked. Overgrowth is +90 HP and 3% max HP.
  SKILL ORDER: W max, E second, Q last. R at 5/9/13. At level 15
    Q, W and E are all rank 4, so the finished fight does not change
    if Q was maxed second. E second is the shield on the way there.
  FIGHT: Cast E on the dive, Q, R so you land as E hits, Q auto the
    carry, bite, keep attacking. Navori does nothing if he is kited.
    Frenzy stays on him when the carry dies. The next bite on whoever
    is left is still the 1.60× maul until the 8 seconds run out.

  TRAPS:
    • A full resist page with no AD leaves the 176% ratio on base AD.
      Heartsteel's one proc does not replace the bites.
    • Deathcap and Nashor with no health fall over while the ultimate
      is still up. Lightning and E scale with AP. The W heal does not.
    • Stacking two spellblades is impossible. Trinity, Dusk, Divine
      and Iceborn share the slot.
    • Sterak and Mantle share the Lifeline. Buying both does nothing
      for the second proc.

MODEL
  Discrete 0.1s. Damage is post-mitigation. Conqueror, passive stacks
  and Lethal Tempo start at zero. Lightning is not on from the first
  attack. Omnivamp is full value against champions. The W heal is
  grievous-cut in the Ignite column. Shields are not.
  Steelcaps / Armored Advance cut 10% of the basic-attack share of
  the physical focus (65% of it). Randuin cuts crits, modeled as 30%
  off 55% of incoming physical. Frozen Heart cuts 25% attack speed
  off that same basic-attack share. Q's stun removes most of the
  physical focus for 1s.
  Shojin's Focused Will percent is not on the stat card this sim
  used, so Shojin is scored on 40 AD, 450 HP and 20 haste. Hullbreaker's
  every-fourth-attack bonus is 160% base AD. Dead Man's crush is not
  modeled. Guinsoo still crits: patch 7.3 removed the crit lock.
  Wit's End no longer heals. Heartsteel lane stacks are 0.7 procs a
  minute after it is finished. Grasp lane stacks are 2.2 procs a minute.
