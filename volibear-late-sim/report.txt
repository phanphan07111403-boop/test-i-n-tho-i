WHAT THE BITE ACTUALLY SCALES WITH
  W1 = 105 + 110% AD + 6% bonus HP
  W2 = W1 × (1.50 + 0.25 per 100 bonus AD) and heals 80 + 20% missing HP
  Bonus HP is inside the multiplier. Bonus AD raises the multiplier.
  Overlord's Bloodmail is 2.5% of bonus HP as AD, plus up to 12% more AD
  when missing health. That AD feeds W, the bite amp, Q (160% bonus AD)
  and R (250% bonus AD). Spirit Visage does not raise the bite. It raises
  the heal, the E shield and the Sterak shield, which is why he gets to bite.

VERDICT

  Buy: Steelcaps → Protoplasm → Navori → Bloodmail → Visage → Cleaver

  WHY THIS IS THE LATE-GAME VOLIBEAR:
  • The wounded bite multiplies bonus health by bonus AD. A tank with the
    same health and no AD leaves that multiplier at 1.50x. This build's
    bite amp at full HP, Conqueror counted only if it is the keystone, is 1.95x.
  • Bloodmail turns that same bonus HP into AD (2.5%) and adds up to 12%
    more AD as he drops, which is when Last Stand and the W heal are both
    online. The AD feeds W, Q (160% bonus AD) and R (250% bonus AD).
  • Protoplasm is the tank Lifeline: bonus HP and a resist-scaled heal
    when he crosses 30% life. It shares the Lifeline slot with Sterak.
  • Visage is +25% healing and shielding. It does not raise the bite.
    It raises the W heal, the E shield and whichever Lifeline he has,
    which is why he is still standing for the next bite.
  • Navori is how the bite comes back inside the ultimate. Each auto
    cuts 15% off the remaining W, E and Q cooldowns, and the 25% crit
    is on the attack itself. He has to be in range and autoing.
  • Protoplasm is first on purpose. The same six items with Navori or
    Cleaver rushed are still dying in the 30:00 focus. The Lifeline is
    why the 26:00 version, Visage just finished, already stands up.
  • R's +525 HP is bonus health for the bite and for both shields. The
    no-ult column is there so a build that only exists for 12 seconds
    does not win.

  Vs full tank (Steelcaps → Heartsteel → Visage → Unending → Jak'Sho → Randuin):
    tank R-dive 4368 in 11.0s (end 39%)   this build 8119 in 11.0s (end 31%).
    The tank lives. The bite does not, because nothing is feeding bonus AD
    into the multiplier. Heartsteel damage does not replace W2.
  Vs AP glass (Ionian → Dusk → Nashor → Deathcap → Shadowflame → Void):
    glass R-dive 6631 in 4.3s, died=True.
    E and lightning scale with AP, and the E shield is real, but W heal
    has no AP ratio on this patch. Focused, the glass build falls over
    before the bites come back. High damage on a target dummy, not in a fight.

  ANTIHEAL:
    With 40% grievous the winner dies at 10.3s.
    If they have Ignite and a Thornmail, prefer Steelcaps → Navori → Cleaver → Bloodmail → Protoplasm → Kaenic
    (8106 damage, lived, end 11%).

  RUNES (baked into the sim):
    Conqueror, Triumph, Legend: Haste, Last Stand
    Revitalize, Overgrowth
    Attack speed, adaptive force, health shard +65
  SKILL ORDER: W max, E second (the %HP bolt), Q last. R at 6/11/16.
  FIGHT: E on the dive spot, Q, R into it so you land as E hits, Q auto,
    bite, keep autoing. Navori does nothing if he is kited. The carry is
    the one he bites. The other champion eats R, E and the lightning chain.

  TRAPS:
    • Heartsteel + pure resists. You are hard to kill and the bite stays
      a 1.5x slap. Bloodmail is the item that makes the health deal damage.
    • Deathcap / Nashor with no health. Lightning looks good for 4 seconds.
    • Trinity instead of Shojin + Bloodmail. Spellblade is 200% base AD
      and does not scale with the bonus AD the bite wants. Trinity is the
      1-item spike, not the 6-item ceiling.
    • Hullbreaker in a 5v5. The solo resist passive is off in this fight.
    • Stacking two Spellblades. Dusk, Trinity and Iceborn share the slot.
      Dusk is the one that also heals (10% AP + 3% bonus HP) and repeats
      on-hits. Take it when the team is magic-heavy, not by default.

  MODEL (so the numbers stay honest):
    Patch 26.21 item stats and Volibear ratios from the wiki (V26.19 kit,
    V26.21 items). 11s focus after the R landing. Incoming at 34:00 is
    980 pre-mit physical (30% pen, 12 lethality, Steelcaps/Randuin
    applied) and 640 pre-mit magic (35% pen, 10 flat). Two champions
    in the fight: the one he is biting, and one other for R/E/Unending/chain.
    Heartsteel assumes about 0.75 procs a minute after it is finished,
    capped at 18. Conqueror starts at 0 and gains 2 stacks per hit, up to
    12. Lethal Tempo ramps to 6 on-hit stacks, then its bolt scales with
    bonus attack speed. Lightning Claws turns on at 5 passive stacks, and
    the hit that reaches 5 is empowered. Attack speed uses Volibear's 0.7
    ratio and the 3.003 cap. Grievous is a separate column, not the ranking.
    Tenacity shortens one 1.15s peel. No flash, no turret, no Elder.
    Search swapped items on the cores until neighbors stopped winning on
    'lived the fight, then damage', then re-scored the finalists on four
    rune pages.
